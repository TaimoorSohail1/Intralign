# Governance Constitution

## Article 19 — Governance Purpose

Governance exists to preserve:
epistemic integrity and trustworthy organizational understanding.

---

## Article 20 — Outcome Integrity Policies

Policies should exist as:
human-readable governance doctrine.

Policies should not feel like:
workflow automation scripting.

---

## Article 21 — Override Visibility

Human overrides remain permissible.

However:
divergence from OSLO understanding must remain visible and historically preservable.

---

## Article 22 — Escalation Proportionality

Governance escalation should scale proportionally to:
- consequence
- confidence degradation
- governance severity
- organizational doctrine

---

## Article 23 — Disagreement Handling

OSLO should primarily act as:
facilitated understanding refinement.

OSLO should:
- expose divergence
- compare evidence
- clarify assumptions
- illuminate tradeoffs

OSLO should avoid:
authoritarian posture.

---

## Article 24 — Intended Reality Governance

Intended Reality itself remains:
governable and inspectable.

OSLO may progressively:
- question
- clarify
- challenge
- propose alternatives

when intended reality appears structurally incoherent.
