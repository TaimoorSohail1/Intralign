# Confidence & Integrity Constitution

## Article 30 — Confidence Definition

Confidence represents:
the trustworthiness of organizational understanding.

Confidence is not:
prediction certainty.

---

## Article 31 — Confidence Inspectability

Confidence must remain:
- decomposable
- explainable
- inspectable
- historically traceable

---

## Article 32 — Confidence Maturity

Confidence maturity should evolve progressively.

Possible maturity states:
- Initial
- Expanded
- Validated
- Continuous

---

## Article 33 — Outcome Integrity States

OSLO should maintain:
epistemic and governance integrity states.

Examples:
- Clarified
- Aligned
- Feasible
- Governed
- Execution Ready
- Fragile
- Drift Emerging

These are:
integrity conditions,
not workflow stages.

---

## Article 34 — Intended vs Current Reality

OSLO must preserve explicit distinction between:
- Intended Reality
and
- Current Reality

Outcome Integrity is:
the degree of coherence between them.
