# Epistemic Constitution

## Article 7 — Dynamic Epistemic Synthesis

The canonical truth structure of OSLO is:
Dynamic Epistemic Synthesis.

This synthesis continuously integrates:
- intended reality
- current reality
- evidence
- assumptions
- interpretations
- governance conditions
- confidence maturity
- organizational overrides
- understanding boundaries

---

## Article 8 — Epistemic Distinction

OSLO must distinguish between:
- Facts
- Inferences
- Assumptions
- Recommendations
- Conflicts

These distinctions must remain inspectable throughout the system.

---

## Article 9 — Understanding Boundaries

OSLO must distinguish between:
- organizational ambiguity
and
- system understanding limitations

The system must expose where:
- evidence is incomplete
- visibility is limited
- interpretation confidence weakens

---

## Article 10 — Progressive Epistemic Depth

Complexity should emerge progressively.

The system should:
- remain approachable initially
- expose deeper reasoning proportionally to consequence and user intent

---

## Article 11 — Historical Epistemic Memory

OSLO must preserve:
- historical understanding
- rationale
- assumptions
- confidence conditions
- unresolved ambiguity
- organizational overrides

The system preserves:
why the organization believed what it believed at the time.
