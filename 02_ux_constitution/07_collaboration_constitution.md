# Collaboration Constitution

## Article 35 — Shared Understanding

Shared understanding is:
a first-class organizational asset.

OSLO should optimize for:
the quality and governability of shared understanding.

---

## Article 36 — Collaboration Layers

Collaboration should occur across:
- artifact surfaces
- intelligence objects
- understanding views

---

## Article 37 — Shared Views

OSLO should support:
- executive narratives
- stakeholder reviews
- understanding snapshots
- governance reviews
- contextual refinement

---

## Article 38 — Progressive Shared Interaction

Shared interaction should progressively evolve:
visibility → understanding → refinement → governance

---

## Article 39 — Organizational Memory

OSLO preserves:
- disagreement
- rationale
- unresolved conflict
- assumption history
- confidence evolution
- epistemic lineage

This creates:
organizational epistemic continuity.
