## 1. Executive Summary

The proposed **Cognitive Responsibility Architecture** is **architecturally sound at its core and should become canonical — with modifications and explicit scoping.** Its central claim — that OSLO is **responsibility-primary** (the cognitive verb is the fundamental unit; layers are a secondary dependency-ordering representation) — is correct, decision-relevant, and survived adversarial validation (Review 003). It resolves the recurring ownership conflicts (recommendations, clarifications, reliability, MRI, Finding/Issue, the implicit goal reference) by giving each an owning responsibility, and it strengthens separation of concerns by making the **engine** the single-responsibility grain.

However, an independent challenge surfaces issues that **prevent ratifying it wholesale as a finished, fully-mature canonical architecture:**
- **(Major) Advise's boundary under-fits Clarification.** The re-grounded boundary "governable candidate **action**" fits Recommendations but strains for **Clarification Requests** (an information request is not an authorizable *action*). The definition must broaden to "candidate **response**" or Clarification must be split out.
- **(Major) Multi-agent readiness is partial.** Naming **Coordinate** is necessary but **not sufficient**: multi-agent OSLO also requires **Authority, Intend, and Retain** to gain multi-principal / multi-goal / shared-state semantics — currently unaddressed. The architecture should **not** claim full multi-agent readiness.
- **(Major) Intend's responsibility-weight is thin in Release 1 and grows with autonomy.** Intend is correctly first-class, but in Release 1 (user-declared, relatively static outcome) it is close to maintained reference-state; its active role (decomposition, drift-comparison, goal-revision interaction with Advise) matures later. This must be characterized precisely to avoid over-claiming.
- **(Major) Taxonomy-proliferation risk.** "Responsibility / Domain / Engine" needs **qualification discipline** or the model will sprawl.
- **(Critical, process) Premature lock-in.** The model has changed substantially across rapid review cycles (Intend/Learn added only in Review 003). Canonicalizing the **entire** model — including its still-maturing edges (Intend depth, Learn, Coordinate, multi-agent semantics) — risks locking in unsettled work.

**The mature governance resolution:** **canonicalize the stable core now; hold the moving edges as provisional.** The core (responsibility-primary; the cognition pipeline Perceive→Retain→Infer→Evaluate→Advise→Disclose→Act; Authority cross-cutting; Adapt emergent; Disclose/Render split; Release 1 ownership) is stable and validated → **ratify as canonical.** The edges (Intend *depth*, Learn, Coordinate, multi-agent semantics, the Advise-boundary fix) → **ratify as provisional/future, explicitly not-yet-canonical**, pending their own maturation. This **unblocks Release 1** (the core + ownership are canonical, so contracts can trace to producers) while being governance-honest about what is not yet finished.

**Recommendation: Ratify with Modifications** (§5).

## 2. Architectural Findings

- **F-1 (confirmed correct).** Responsibility-primary is the right organizing principle — it is decision-relevant (governs where new capabilities go and how ownership conflicts resolve) and prevents the "orphan responsibility" failure mode that caused the conflicts.
- **F-2 (Major).** The **Advise boundary** ("governable candidate action") is **mis-sized for Clarification.** A Clarification Request is a candidate **information request**, governed for *exposure* but **not authorized as an action**. The unifying boundary should be **"governable candidate response"** (action *or* information request) — or Clarification should be a **sibling responsibility/engine** with an exposure-only governance relationship.
- **F-3 (Major).** **Multi-agent ≠ Coordinate alone.** Coordinate (arbitration) is necessary but multi-agent also requires **multi-principal Authority**, **multi/conflicting-goal Intend**, and **shared/partitioned Retain**. These are **unaddressed**; the architecture's "no future rework" implication for multi-agent is **overstated**.
- **F-4 (Major).** **Intend is correctly first-class but under-characterized.** It is the responsibility owning the **maintained, decomposed outcome reference** (distinct from Retain's storage and Evaluate's drift-computation). In Release 1 it is **thin** (reference-holding); its active role (decomposition, goal-revision via Advise→Authority→Intend) **grows with autonomy.** Must be stated precisely.
- **F-5 (Major).** **Taxonomy discipline missing.** Without explicit qualification criteria, "Responsibility/Domain/Engine" can proliferate. The model must define what *is* a responsibility (a distinct cognitive verb in the loop) vs an engine (a single capability) vs a service (non-cognitive).
- **F-6 (Minor).** **Hierarchy imprecision.** "Responsibility → Domain → Engine" implies three levels; in truth **Domain = a Responsibility with >1 engine** (a cardinality state, not a separate level). The real composition is **Responsibility(=Domain) ▷ Engine.** And **Plane** (Authority) and **Layer** are both **representations** (cross-cutting view; dependency-ordering view) — only **Service** is a different kind (non-cognitive support). So the orthogonal axes are **Representation** (Layer/Plane) and **Support** (Service).
- **F-7 (Minor).** **Governance plane has defined gates.** Authority is correctly cross-cutting, but it acts at **specific touchpoints** (constrain-at-generation; govern-at-exposure; authorize-at-actuation) — a plane *with gates*, not a diffuse cloud; worth stating for precision.
- **F-8 (Critical, process).** **Stability/maturity for canonicalization.** The model changed materially in the last two reviews; its **core is now stable** (survived adversarial validation) but its **edges are still maturing.** Canonicalizing the *whole* prematurely is a governance risk; canonicalizing the *core* with *provisional edges* is the correct move.
- **F-9 (confirmed correct).** **Adapt-emergent, Authority-cross-cutting, Disclose/Render split, Reliability→Evaluate, MRI→Disclose, Resolution-Paths-presentation-only** are all sound and resolve real prior gaps.

## 3. Governance Decisions (Questions A–I)

**A. Layer / Responsibility / Hybrid?** → **Hybrid, Responsibility-Primary.** Pure Layer is wrong (mis-models the loop, cross-cutting, bidirectional governance; it hid Advise). Pure Responsibility discards the *valid* dependency-ordering discipline layers encode. **Adopt responsibility-primary with layering retained as a dependency-ordering representation** — *and* with taxonomy discipline (F-5) so the principle doesn't sprawl.

**B. Is the responsibility set complete?** → **Substantially complete; one Major definitional fix and explicit future-scoping required.**
- *Missing:* none *severe* (Review 003 fixed Intend/Learn). **Caveat:** multi-agent needs more than Coordinate (F-3); goal-revision is an Advise→Intend interaction to name (Minor).
- *Redundant:* none.
- *Merge:* **No** — keep Infer/Evaluate separate (explainability/replay); keep Disclose/Act separate (meaning-to-humans vs coordination-in-systems).
- *Split:* **Yes (Major)** — **Advise** mixes Recommendations (candidate actions) and Clarifications (candidate information requests) under a boundary ("candidate action") that fits only the former. **Broaden to "candidate response" or split Clarification** (F-2).

**C. Governance as cross-cutting Authority Plane?** → **Yes — correct.** Modeling Governance as a cross-cutting Authority Plane (constrains inputs, governs outputs, **generates nothing**) preserves the authority/cognition separation that pure-sequential placement violated. **Refinement (F-7):** state its **defined gates** (constrain-at-generation, govern-at-exposure, authorize-at-actuation) — a plane with gates, not diffuse. **Do not** return it to a sequential stage.

**D. Intend first-class?** → **Yes — but precisely characterized (F-4).** Without a maintained outcome reference, drift/alignment (OSLO's central output) is undefined, so Intend is **necessary** and first-class. It owns the **reference and its decomposition/maintenance** — distinct from Retain (storage) and Evaluate (consumes the reference). **It is thin in Release 1 and deepens with autonomy;** state this so it is not over-claimed as a heavy per-cycle process today.

**E. Is Advisory a true responsibility?** → **Yes — a responsibility (a domain of engines), not merely an engine/capability.** It is a whole cognitive stage (the universal option-generation step) with a distinct governance relationship and multiple engines (Recommendation, Clarification). **Caveat (F-2):** its *boundary definition* needs the candidate-response fix; the *responsibility status* is confirmed.

**F. Is the hierarchy correct?** → **Directionally yes, with precision (F-6).** Correct to: **Responsibility (primary) ▷ Engine** (a Responsibility with >1 engine is termed a **Domain** — a cardinality state, not a separate level); **orthogonal axes = Representation (Layer, Plane) and Support (Service).** Restate the hierarchy this way to remove the false three-level implication and to clarify that Plane/Layer are *representations* while Service is *support*.

**G. Supports all futures without rework?** → **Release 1 / Execution Intelligence / Outcome Orchestration / Agent Governance / Governed Automation: yes** (the corrected model carries these; Outcome Orchestration *given* Intend). **Multi-Agent Coordination: partially (F-3)** — Coordinate is named and additive, but full multi-agent requires **multi-principal Authority, multi-goal Intend, shared Retain**, which are **not yet specified.** **Do not claim full multi-agent readiness without rework.**

**H. Unresolved risks** (classified):
- **Critical:** **F-8 premature lock-in** — canonicalizing the *entire* still-maturing model. *Consequence:* locking unsettled edges (Intend depth, Learn, Coordinate, multi-agent) into canon, forcing future supersession. *Mitigation:* canonicalize core; hold edges provisional.
- **Major:** **F-2** (Advise boundary vs Clarification) — *consequence:* ambiguous ownership/governance for clarifications in contracts; **F-3** (multi-agent under-readiness) — *consequence:* future rework if multi-agent is built on the single-principal model as-is; **F-4** (Intend under-characterization) — *consequence:* over/under-scoped Intend contracts; **F-5** (taxonomy sprawl) — *consequence:* responsibility/engine proliferation, eroding clarity.
- **Minor:** **F-6** (hierarchy precision), **F-7** (governance gates), naming (Disclose vs Communication; Authority vs Governance), goal-revision interaction, distributed self-verification.

**I. Approve as canonical?** → **Ratify with Modifications** (option 2). Not "as-is" (Major findings F-2/F-3/F-4 + Critical F-8 require modification/scoping); not "reject" (the core is sound, validated, and resolves real gaps). **Ratify the stable core as canonical; ratify the edges as provisional; apply the §4 modifications.**

## 4. Required Modifications

- **M-1 (Critical, scoping).** **Canonicalize the stable core now; mark the edges provisional.** *Canonical:* responsibility-primary principle; the pipeline **Perceive → Retain → Infer → Evaluate → Advise → Disclose → Act**; **Authority** cross-cutting (generates nothing); **Adapt** emergent; **Disclose/Render** split; **Reliability→Evaluate, MRI→Disclose, Resolution-Paths presentation-only**; Release 1 ownership (Update Spec §3). *Provisional/future (not yet canonical):* **Intend depth** beyond reference-holding; **Learn**; **Coordinate**; **multi-agent semantics**; the Advise-boundary fix.
- **M-2 (Major).** **Fix the Advise boundary** to **"governable candidate response"** (action *or* information request), or **split Clarification** as a sibling with an exposure-only governance relationship. (Choose before clarification contracts are generated.)
- **M-3 (Major).** **State Intend precisely** — owner of the maintained, decomposed outcome reference; thin in Release 1, deepens with autonomy; goal-revision is an Advise→Authority→Intend interaction (future).
- **M-4 (Major).** **Do not claim full multi-agent readiness.** Mark **multi-principal Authority, multi-goal Intend, shared Retain** as **open** alongside Coordinate.
- **M-5 (Major).** **Add taxonomy qualification discipline** — define Responsibility (distinct cognitive verb in the loop) vs Engine (single capability) vs Service (non-cognitive support); new constructs classified before adoption.
- **M-6 (Minor).** **Restate the hierarchy** as Responsibility(=Domain by cardinality) ▷ Engine; orthogonal axes = Representation (Layer/Plane) + Support (Service). **State Authority's defined gates.** Resolve **naming** (Disclose vs Communication; Authority vs Governance; Perceive vs Context Plane) at owner preference.

**None of M-1…M-6 blocks Release 1 contract generation** — the canonical core (M-1) gives every Release 1 element an owning responsibility; M-2 affects only clarification-contract wording; M-3/M-4/M-5/M-6 are refinement/future-scoping.

## 5. Final Recommendation

**RATIFY WITH MODIFICATIONS.**

Adopt the **Cognitive Responsibility Architecture** as OSLO's **canonical architecture** — **responsibility-primary**, with the validated core pipeline and ownership — **subject to the §4 modifications**, chief among them **M-1 (canonicalize the stable core; hold the maturing edges provisional)** and **M-2 (fix the Advise/Clarification boundary).** This (a) **unblocks Release 1** — the canonical core gives every cognition-owned capability a documented producer, so Contract Inventory generation may proceed for all Release 1 elements; (b) **resolves the recurring ownership conflicts**; (c) **strengthens separation of concerns** at the engine grain; and (d) is **governance-honest** about what is not yet finished (multi-agent semantics, Learn, Coordinate, Intend depth), preventing premature lock-in.

**Why not Ratify as-is:** F-2 (Advise boundary), F-3 (multi-agent), F-4 (Intend), and F-8 (premature lock-in) require modification/scoping before the *whole* model is canon. **Why not Reject:** the core is correct, validated, resolves real gaps, and rejecting would leave OSLO with the broken layer-primary model and its orphaned responsibilities. **The correct governance act is to ratify the validated core, scope the immature edges as provisional, and proceed.**

**GOV-ARCH-001 disposition (recommended to owner):** Cognitive Responsibility Architecture → **canonical (core), provisional (edges)**, per §4. Upon owner ratification, the prior layer-primary baseline is read as the dependency-ordering representation, and Release 1 Contract Inventory generation may begin.
