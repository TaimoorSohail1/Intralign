# Confidence & Understanding Model

## Confidence Definition

Confidence represents:
the trustworthiness of organizational understanding.

Not:
prediction certainty.

---

# Confidence Drivers

Potential dimensions:
- clarity
- alignment
- feasibility
- evidence density
- interpretation stability
- governance integrity
- assumption stability

---

# Confidence Communication Doctrine

Confidence should be:
- inspectable
- explainable
- decomposable
- historically traceable

Confidence should never appear:
magical or absolute.

---

# Understanding Evolution

OSLO tracks:
- how understanding changed
- why it changed
- confidence movement
- assumption evolution
- governance impacts

This creates:
understanding evolution over time.

---

# Stability vs Movement

OSLO should feel:
structurally calm,
intellectually evolving.

Stable structures:
- workspace
- navigation
- understanding shell

Evolution appears:
where meaning changes.

---

# Emotional Interpretation

Users should feel:
- grounded
- strategically augmented
- structurally aware
- calm under complexity
