# Draft Constitutional Principles

1. Preserve trustworthy organizational understanding under dynamic conditions.

2. Understanding before execution.

3. Outcome integrity is the degree of coherence between intended and current reality.

4. Confidence must remain inspectable and explainable.

5. Human judgment remains authoritative.

6. Divergence must remain visible and governable.

7. Artifacts support understanding.

8. Complexity should emerge progressively.

9. Uncertainty should remain structurally visible.

10. Governance protects epistemic integrity.

11. OSLO augments judgment rather than replacing it.

12. Shared understanding is a first-class organizational asset.

13. Intelligence visibility scales with uncertainty and consequence.

14. Intended reality itself is governable and inspectable.

15. Historical epistemic context must remain preservable.

16. Dynamic epistemic synthesis is the canonical truth structure of the system.

17. Organizational cognition evolves from individual understanding to orchestration infrastructure.

18. Outcome integrity supersedes workflow optimization.

19. Conversation supports understanding, not vice versa.

20. Calm strategic augmentation should define the emotional posture of the system.
