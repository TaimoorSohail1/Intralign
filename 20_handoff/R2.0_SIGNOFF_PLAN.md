## 1. SCOPING RULING — what R2.0 sign-off binds to

**R2.0 sign-off binds to the 20 criterion ids of the production-gating subset, measured on the
deployed build. Corpus integrity is NOT a release gate.**

Unresolved citations, graduation debt, index markers, register rows and gate refactors are **corpus
debt**. They are real work and they are tracked elsewhere. They **do not gate R2.0 sign-off**, they
may not be added to this plan, and no R2.0 row may be marked blocked because of them.

*Why this exists:* in the seven days to 2026-09-02, 42 commits landed on `main` touching 41 files —
19 in `20_handoff`, 11 in `00_owner`, 9 in `tools`/`.github`, and **zero application files**. The
apparatus that would certify R2.0 grew; R2.0 did not move. This ruling separates the two clocks.

⚠️ **R2.0 may only be described as "validated against the production-gating subset." Never
"validated."** The subset is 20 ids of a larger set; the remainder is deferred by #295, not passed.

## 5. SEQUENCE — the order that minimises repeat work

1. **Deploy the build carrying #280's identity fix.** N-5 closes only when the identity is
   *reported*, not when the deploy happens. Six obligations and B1/B2/B3 cannot be measured at all
   until a build is deployed — this is the gate on everything below.
2. **One staging measurement session, not six errands.** GT-107 · GT-106/110 · N-6 · N-9 · N-10 and
   B1/B2/B3 are all observable in a single pass. Converts `untested` into a state.
3. **Fix the four measured-failing criteria** — N-1 · N-4 · N-7 · N-8. RED conditions are already
   known; these need fixing, not investigating.
4. **Resolve the two `unclassified` rows** — GT-35 and N-4 — into the vocabulary.
5. **Re-run §4 and sign off** when `fail` and `untested` are both zero.

⚠️ Steps 1 and 2 are blocked on `#279 → #280 → deploy`, which is engineering's queue. Step 3 is not
blocked and can start immediately.
