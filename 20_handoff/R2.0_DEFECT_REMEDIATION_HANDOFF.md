### 3.3 · §4 Attestation on the two self-service grounding paths — ⚠️ RULED 2026-08-24
`Confirm — it holds` and `Let OSLO ask you a question` both ground with **no basis recorded**, while
`Ask for evidence` correctly withholds grounding until answered and attributes it. ⚠️ **The product
promises *"reviewer attestations are retained in project history"* and collects none to retain.**

⚠️ **CORRECTION to this item as originally written.** It says `_CONFIRM_BASES` "is wired to neither".
**It is wired to three other call sites** — `confirmBasis()`, `groundStmt()` and `railConfirm()` — and
the `it.attest` branch already ships a full two-step **basis → evidence** flow. **The attestation UI
exists.** What is missing is that the rail's primary CTA calls `quickConfirm(k)` →
`itemAct(k,'confirm')`, with no basis and no evidence. **So this is a routing defect, not a design gap
— nothing new is invented.**

**RULED — the basis REPLACES the confirm button; it does not precede it.**
Instead of `✓ Confirm — it holds`, the row offers the three existing `_CONFIRM_BASES` directly, under
*"It holds because…"*. ⚠️ **Same tap count as today** — the user makes one click either way — and the
basis, the attester and the timestamp are recorded. `It doesn't hold` and `Ask for evidence` are
unchanged.

⚠️ **Rejected: adding a basis picker in front of the existing confirm button.** It taxes the single
most repeated action in the core loop by one tap, which is the friction this item was escalated for.

⚠️ **Acknowledged cost, ruled acceptable:** replacing the button removes the one unambiguous "yes" and
requires the user to classify before agreeing. That is a choice cost even where it is not a tap cost.
**`I've verified this directly` is the catch-all** — no honest user is blocked.

**Class: Altering · its own gated PR · reuse `_CONFIRM_BASES`, do not mint new strings.**

#### 3.3b · The clarify path — ⚠️ RULED 2026-08-24 · no interaction change
