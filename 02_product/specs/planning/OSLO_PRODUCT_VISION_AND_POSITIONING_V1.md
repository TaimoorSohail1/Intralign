## 2. Product Vision — OSLO (the 3–5 year future state)
**What goes here:** what OSLO becomes if it wins; the world with OSLO in it.
**Grounded direction (from canon):** OSLO is a **Planning/Outcome Intelligence system** that turns raw project artifacts into an evidence-grounded, continuously-recomputed *understanding* — surfacing clarity, alignment, feasibility, issues, and **honestly-qualified confidence**, so organizations preserve **outcome integrity** (coherence between Intended and Current Reality — DL-002) as conditions change. It **advises; it never acts or asserts certainty.**
**`[OWNER TO COMPLETE]`** — the aspirational end-state framing (the "OSLO becomes the ___ for ___" statement).

## 3. The Problem / Why Now
**What goes here:** the pain OSLO removes; why this is solvable now (AI-native).
**Grounded fragments:** organizations lose understanding as plans evolve; planning artifacts drift from reality; confidence is asserted, not evidenced. AI-native synthesis + continuous recompute now make trustworthy, recomputable understanding feasible.
**`[OWNER TO COMPLETE]`** — the sharpened problem statement + the "why now" wedge.

## 4. Category & Positioning
**What goes here:** the category OSLO creates or enters; the alternatives; the wedge.
**Grounded angle (defensible from canon):** OSLO's differentiator is **epistemic honesty** — it never fakes certainty, never asserts project "health," never acts autonomously; understanding is **Attested + recomputable**, confidence is **maturity not probability**. This is a *trust-first* posture distinct from typical "AI does it for you" tools. The Visual/brand positioning the owner has used: *strategic · executive · AI-native · trustworthy · premium · outcome-focused* (CHG-068).
**`[OWNER TO COMPLETE]`** — the **category name** (creating vs entering), the **competitive set / alternatives**, and the one-line **wedge**.
