# Design Risks

## Risk 1 — Too Much Complexity Too Early
Mitigation:
progressive disclosure and PLG separation.

## Risk 2 — Chat-Centric Drift
Mitigation:
keep chat in companion panel, not primary nav.

## Risk 3 — Dashboard Drift
Mitigation:
center Understanding, not metrics alone.

## Risk 4 — Confidence Misinterpretation
Mitigation:
confidence maturity and inspectability.

## Risk 5 — Governance Feels Bureaucratic
Mitigation:
human-readable doctrine and proportional escalation.

## Risk 6 — Freemium Too Weak
Mitigation:
include deep refinement in free tier.

## Risk 7 — Artifacts Dominate
Mitigation:
artifacts remain supporting surfaces.

## Risk 8 — OSLO Feels Too Abstract
Mitigation:
familiar artifacts and concrete recommendations.

## Risk 9 — AI Feels Too Authoritative
Mitigation:
human authority + visible divergence.

## Risk 10 — Noisy Intelligence
Mitigation:
ambient awareness + attention queue + rare escalation.
