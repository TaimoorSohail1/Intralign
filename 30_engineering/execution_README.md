# Execution

This domain holds operational tracking content. Content is operational rather than canonical.

## Content

- `implementation_tracking/` — Implementation backlog, open questions, design risks.

## Authority Posture

Updates to Execution content do not require Proposal-Review-Decision provided they do not assert doctrinal or constitutional positions. When Execution surfaces a doctrinal question, the question is escalated to the Revision Backlog (`01_governance/backlog/`) rather than answered in Execution.

## Future Subdirectories

The following operational tracking domains may be added incrementally when content exists. No empty directories are pre-created.

- roadmap
- initiatives
- milestones
