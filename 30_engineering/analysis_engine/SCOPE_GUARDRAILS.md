## Required affirmations (canonical)

- Findings are **descriptive**; Recommendations are **advisory**.
- Confidence is **derived from CAF + Reliability**; never primary; never bare.
- Prior outputs are **superseded, not deleted**.
- The **user retains authority**.
- No new entities, states, events, or capabilities beyond the Data/State/Event models and Canonical Scope.

## Boundary behavior (derived)

- If a required detail is unsupported by the corpus, write **`TBD – Owner Decision Required`** — do not invent it.
- If sources conflict, apply the authority order (Planning Intelligence first) and record the conflict.
- Proposals (e.g., global skeleton, ingestion envelope, claim attributes) are clearly tagged `proposal` and routed to OPEN_DECISIONS for owner ratification — never silently adopted as canonical.

## Governance note (canonical)

Per repository governance, this pack is **non-canonical** and subject to owner review before adoption. It introduces no doctrine; it consolidates and operationalizes existing, ratified specifications and clearly-labeled proposals.
