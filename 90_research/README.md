# Research

This domain holds non-canonical source material. Per DL-033, Source Material informs but does not bind.

## Content

- `transcripts/` — Raw transcripts of ideation, workshops, and design sessions.
- `historical_artifacts/` — Preserved historical files (e.g., the Constitutional Principles Draft per DL-035; the historical implementation specs index and README per DL-037 Clarification #8).

## Authority

Per DL-033, Source Material is **non-canonical**. It cannot be cited as authority in Proposals. Contributors may consult Research content for interpretive context, but resolution of ambiguity requires a Proposal targeting canonical content.

## Preservation Posture

The Research domain exists to preserve original understanding across change. Files may be added freely; files may not be removed without a Decision.
