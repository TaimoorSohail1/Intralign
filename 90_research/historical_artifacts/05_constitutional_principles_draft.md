# Constitutional Principles Draft (Historical Artifact)

## Historical Artifact Header

**Status:** Non-canonical Source Material (per DL-033; reclassified by DL-035).

**Provenance:** This file was originally located at `01_doctrine_ontology/12_constitutional_principles_draft.md` and was drafted as a candidate enumeration of constitutional principles during the OSLO codification process. It was labeled as a Draft and was never ratified.

**Pre-Ratification Status:** Before the ratification of DL-033 (Doctrine-Centered Repository Architecture), the file sat at the Doctrine layer. The doctrine-centered architecture established that Doctrine > Constitution > Implementation Specifications. As a Drafted doctrinal document, the file would nominally have outranked ratified Constitution Articles where they conflicted. A pre-ratification annotation in the file acknowledged this instability.

**Disposition of Draft Principle 17 (per DL-034):** Draft Principle 17 ("Organizational cognition evolves from individual understanding to orchestration infrastructure") was absorbed by Doctrine 02 (Organizational Cognition Model) via DL-034. Its substantive content is canonized through Doctrine 02's four-stage Cognition Scope arc of the OSLO Evolution Framework and is recorded in `01_governance/decisions/rb_003_disposition.md`.

**Disposition of Remaining 19 Principles (per DL-035):** The remaining 19 principles are dispositioned by reclassification. The file is relocated from the Doctrine layer to the Source Material layer (`00_raw_transcript/`) and reclassified as non-canonical. Of the 19, 18 are substantively represented by Constitution Articles 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 18, 19, 21, 24, 25, 28, 31, 35, and 45, which retain their operational authority. Draft Principle 18 ("Outcome integrity supersedes workflow optimization") is substantively captured by Article 2 (Understanding Before Execution) and Constitutional Drift Warning 2 (Workflow Drift).

**Citation Rules (per DL-033):** Source Material informs but does not bind. This file cannot be cited as authority. Contributors should cite the corresponding Constitution Articles when referencing the principles below.

**Authorizing Decision:** DL-035 (Convert Constitutional Principles Draft to Historical Artifact per RB-010). Full disposition recorded in `01_governance/decisions/rb_010_disposition.md`.

## Principles (Original Drafted Form)

1. Preserve trustworthy organizational understanding under dynamic conditions.

2. Understanding before execution.

3. Outcome integrity is the degree of coherence between intended and current reality.

4. Confidence must remain inspectable and explainable.

5. Human judgment remains authoritative.

6. Divergence must remain visible and governable.

7. Artifacts support understanding.

8. Complexity should emerge progressively.

9. Uncertainty should remain structurally visible.

10. Governance protects epistemic integrity.

11. OSLO augments judgment rather than replacing it.

12. Shared understanding is a first-class organizational asset.

13. Intelligence visibility scales with uncertainty and consequence.

14. Intended reality itself is governable and inspectable.

15. Historical epistemic context must remain preservable.

16. Dynamic epistemic synthesis is the canonical truth structure of the system.

17. Organizational cognition evolves from individual understanding to orchestration infrastructure.

18. Outcome integrity supersedes workflow optimization.

19. Conversation supports understanding, not vice versa.

20. Calm strategic augmentation should define the emotional posture of the system.
