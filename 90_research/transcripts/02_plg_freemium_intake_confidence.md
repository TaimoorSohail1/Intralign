# OSLO UI Raw Transcript — Part 2
## PLG, Freemium, Intake, Confidence, and Activation

## Q21 — Creating a New Outcome Space
**Question:** How should users begin when creating a new Outcome Space?

**Decision:** Multi-input intake.

**Added requirement:** Include buttons that auto-paste sample project description text.

**Rationale:** Users should be able to bring prompts, documents, transcripts, exports, and examples.

---

## Q22 — Post-Intake Transition
**Question:** After intake, should users immediately enter What OSLO Understands, or first see a Project MRI reveal?

**Recommendation:** Project MRI / analysis reveal first, then transition into What OSLO Understands.

**Rationale:** The reveal creates the “OSLO moment.”

---

## Q23 — Overriding OSLO Interpretations
**Question:** Should users be able to override OSLO interpretations?

**Recommendation:** Yes, both artifacts and OSLO understanding can be edited, with governed distinction between source truth and interpreted truth.

**Rationale:** Users can correct interpretation without corrupting source lineage.

---

## Q24 — Automatic Improvements
**Question:** Should OSLO automatically make improvements?

**Recommendation:** Limited autonomous improvement with one-click approval.

**Rationale:** Diagnosis without relief is weak, but invisible mutation is dangerous.

---

## Q25 — Future Outcome Simulations
**Question:** Should users see future outcome simulations?

**Recommendation:** Yes, guided scenario exploration, not predictive certainty.

**Rationale:** OSLO should help users explore consequences without pretending to predict the future.

---

## Q26 — Issue Organization
**Question:** How should OSLO organize issues?

**Recommendation:** Multi-dimensional issue system with default sort by outcome impact.

**Filters include:**
- severity
- confidence impact
- artifact
- clarity/alignment/feasibility
- owner
- time
- recommendation status

---

## Q27 — Working Memory / Scratchpad
**Question:** Should OSLO support working memory?

**Recommendation:** Yes, governed Working Memory, not generic notes.

**Rationale:** Users need a place for incomplete, speculative, uncertain thoughts that can later be promoted into canonical structures.

---

## Q28 — AI Personas or Single Identity
**Question:** Should OSLO support multiple AI personas?

**Recommendation:** One OSLO identity with dynamic expertise modes underneath.

**Rationale:** Multiple visible personas create fragmentation.

---

## Q29 — Knowledge / Relationship Map
**Question:** Should OSLO show a relationship map?

**Recommendation:** Yes, as an advanced intelligence lens, not primary workspace.

**Recommended label:** Outcome Map.

---

## Q30 — Saved Workspace Layouts
**Question:** Should OSLO support saved workspace layouts or modes?

**Recommendation:** Yes, adaptive defaults plus user customization.

**Examples:**
- Planning
- Optimization
- Executive Review
- Governance
- Scenario

---

## 60-Second Flow Clarification
**Critical clarification:** The 60-second flow is different from the core user flow.

**Distinction:**
- 60-second flow = PLG activation experience
- daily workspace = ongoing outcome optimization environment

---

## PLG Clarification
**User clarified:** The 60-second flow is a PLG freemium onboarding experience. It should onboard first-time users, activate them, then transition them into ongoing usage and eventually paid tiers.

**Implication:** The 60-second flow is acquisition, activation, trust formation, and conversion setup.

---

## Q31 — Artifact Visibility During PLG Flow
**Question:** During the 60-second PLG flow, should users immediately see artifacts?

**Recommendation:** Keep artifacts mostly hidden during the 60-second PLG experience, then progressively reveal them.

**Rationale:** Minute one should deliver insight, not work.

---

## Q32 — Locked Capability Reminders
**Question:** During freemium usage, should OSLO continuously show locked features?

**Recommendation:** Contextual expansion prompts, not persistent upgrade wallpaper.

**Rationale:** Upgrade prompts should appear when users already understand value.

---

## Q33 — Confidence Scores in Freemium
**Question:** Should freemium show raw confidence scores or confidence trend/improvement framing?

**User clarification:** Freemium should include both fast-pass confidence and deeper confidence refinement.

**Recommendation:** Use confidence maturity model.

**Stages:**
- Initial
- Expanded
- Validated
- Continuous

**Important correction:** Deep refinement should be included in freemium, with constraints around frequency, scale, monitoring, automation, collaboration, and integrations.

---

## Q34 — Compare Current vs Previous Understanding
**Question:** Should OSLO allow comparison of current vs previous understanding?

**Recommendation:** Yes, and not merely version history.

**Rationale:** OSLO should preserve the evolution of meaning, not just document versions.

---

## Q35 — Urgency Communication
**Question:** How should OSLO communicate urgency?

**Recommendation:** Combined system with hierarchy:
1. Business consequence
2. Confidence impact
3. Severity
4. Suggested action

**Rationale:** OSLO should communicate why something matters to the outcome.
