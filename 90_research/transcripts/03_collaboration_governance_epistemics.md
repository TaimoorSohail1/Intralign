# OSLO UI Raw Transcript — Part 3
## Collaboration, Governance, Epistemics, and Enterprise Behavior

## Q36 — Collaborative Multi-User Interactions
**Question:** Should OSLO support collaborative interactions inside artifacts and understanding views?

**Decision:** Yes.

**Refinement:** Collaboration should exist at three layers:
1. Artifact-level collaboration
2. Intelligence-level collaboration
3. Understanding-level collaboration

**Additional requirement:** Users should be able to share views such as Intent View or Context View for document/artifact-level review.

---

## Q37 — Assumptions as First-Class Objects
**Question:** Should OSLO treat assumptions as first-class objects?

**User clarification:** Assumptions are part of ambiguity/clarity and have epistemic state.

**Refined recommendation:** Assumptions should be first-class epistemic entities internally, visible and inspectable, but not necessarily standalone top-level user-facing artifact domains.

---

## Q38 — Notifications and Attention Management
**Question:** How should OSLO handle notifications?

**Recommendation:** Multi-layer system:
1. Ambient awareness
2. Attention Queue
3. Escalations
4. Digest / evolution summaries

**Principle:** Calm intelligence, escalating only when outcome integrity is threatened.

---

## Q39 — Outcome Health Thresholds
**Question:** Should OSLO support explicit outcome health thresholds?

**Recommendation:** Yes, progressively introduced.

**Phases:**
- implicit thresholds in freemium
- guided thresholds in paid tiers
- organizational governance thresholds in enterprise

---

## Q40 — When OSLO Is Uncertain
**Question:** What should OSLO do when uncertain?

**Recommendation:** Combined epistemic handling:
- expose uncertainty
- ask guided clarification questions
- offer competing interpretations
- provide constrained recommendations

**Rationale:** OSLO should operationalize uncertainty rather than hide it.

---

## Q41 — Visual Reasoning Exposure
**Question:** Should OSLO expose internal reasoning visually?

**Recommendation:** Expose reasoning progressively and inspectably, but do not expose raw chain-of-thought style cognition.

**Layers:**
1. Conclusion
2. Why
3. Evidence
4. Interpretation Model
5. Understanding Evolution

---

## Q42 — Organizational Adaptation
**Question:** Should OSLO allow organizations to define terminology, models, confidence dimensions, rules, and artifacts?

**Recommendation:** Opinionated core framework plus controlled organizational adaptation.

**Principle:** Stable canonical OSLO model with organizational mapping and policy layers.

---

## Q43 — Visual Representation of Uncertainty
**Question:** How should OSLO visually represent uncertainty and epistemic weakness?

**Recommendation:** Combined system:
- explicit epistemic labels
- confidence visualization
- structural visual cues
- severity sparingly
- evolution indicators

**Principle:** Epistemic state should feel inspectable, not merely color-coded.

**Question:** Should OSLO lean toward simplicity or accuracy?

**Recommendation:** Progressive epistemic depth.

## Q54 — Understanding Boundaries
**Question:** Should OSLO communicate areas it may not fully understand?

**Recommendation:** Yes.

**Important distinction:**
- Ambiguity = organization lacks clarity
- Understanding Boundary = OSLO lacks sufficient evidence or visibility

## Q55 — Outcome Integrity Policies
**Question:** Should OSLO support explicit Outcome Integrity Policies?

**Recommendation:** Yes, as human-readable governance doctrine, not rule engines.

**Examples:**
- Execution cannot begin without validated ownership.
- Confidence below 70 requires executive review.
- Assumptions older than 30 days require revalidation.
