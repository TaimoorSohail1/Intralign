## Most Important Emerging Doctrines

- OSLO is a Cursor-like AI-native IDE for Outcome Orchestration.
- The primary object is an Outcome Space.
- The core experience centers on What OSLO Understands.
- Artifacts are familiar work surfaces; OSLO cognition is orchestration-native underneath.
- Confidence is a persistent epistemic shell, not a report.
- OSLO should preserve trustworthy organizational understanding under dynamic conditions.
- OSLO should distinguish facts, inferences, assumptions, recommendations, and conflicts.
- OSLO should expose uncertainty progressively and proportionally to consequence.
- OSLO should distinguish Intended Reality from Current Reality.
- Outcome Integrity is coherence between intended reality and current reality.
- OSLO’s unit of truth is dynamic epistemic synthesis.
