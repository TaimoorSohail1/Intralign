## Repository Owner Guidance

The decision applies seven owner-supplied principles:

1. The four progression models are not collapsed into a single progression.
2. The four progressions are treated as distinct but correlated axes: Cognition Scope, Product Identity, Trust Gradient, Execution Depth.
3. Doctrine remains authoritative over Constitution where stage labels or counts conflict (consistent with DL-033).
4. Doctrine 02 is the canonical Cognition Scope arc.
5. Portfolio Cognition is treated as a provisional long-term capability, not a ratified stage, until separately governed.
6. Doctrine 09's "Governed Organizational Cognition" label prevails over Article 40's "Governance Infrastructure" label.
7. Draft Principle 17 is treated as absorbed by Doctrine 02 for purposes of RB-010.

## Rationale

The four progressions describe distinct facets of OSLO's evolution — cognition scope, product identity, trust grant, execution interaction — that are conceptually independent but empirically correlated. Collapsing them loses analytical resolution; treating them as a single arc misrepresents the doctrine. The owner privileges fidelity to doctrinal source material; under DL-033, doctrinal precedence governs label conflicts. Portfolio Cognition exists as an anticipated capability in Doctrine 10 but is not a stage in Doctrine 02; the four-stage Cognition Scope arc therefore prevails until Portfolio Cognition is separately governed.

## Ratified Taxonomy — The OSLO Evolution Framework

The framework comprises four distinct but correlated axes. The axes are independently measurable. The four progressions converge structurally at terminal Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure (or, for Trust Gradient, Orchestration Authority — a trust-dimensional articulation of the same endpoint).

### Axis A — Cognition Scope

**Canonical Source:** Doctrine 02 (Organizational Cognition Model)

**Stages (ratified, four):**
1. Individual Cognition
2. Shared Understanding
3. Governed Organizational Cognition
4. Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure

**Subject:** Scale of cognition (single user to organization-wide governed cognition).

**Provisional addition (not ratified):** Portfolio Cognition. Article 44's five-stage arc that places Portfolio Cognition between organizational cognition and orchestration infrastructure is provisional. Doctrine 02's four-stage arc prevails.

### Axis B — Product Identity

**Canonical Source:** Doctrine 09 (PLG & Product Evolution Strategy)

**Stages (ratified, four):**
1. Planning and Understanding Intelligence
2. Strategic Cognition Environment
3. Governed Organizational Cognition
4. Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure

**Subject:** How organizations identify and position OSLO.

**Ratified label at Stage 3:** "Governed Organizational Cognition" (Doctrine 09). Article 40's "Governance Infrastructure" label at Stage 3 is provisional and superseded by the doctrinal label.

### Axis C — Trust Gradient

**Canonical Source:** Doctrine 09 (Trust Evolution); aligned with Article 41

**Steps (ratified, four):**
1. OSLO Observations
2. OSLO Recommendations
3. OSLO Operational Influence
4. OSLO Orchestration Authority

**Subject:** Agency organizations grant to OSLO.

**Status:** Doctrine and Constitution are aligned. No reconciliation required.

### Axis D — Execution Depth

**Canonical Source:** Doctrine 10 (Execution & Orchestration Maturity); aligned with Article 43

**Phases (ratified, five, zero-indexed):**
- Phase 0: Execution-State Ingestion
- Phase 1: Observational Intelligence
- Phase 2: Guided Optimization
- Phase 3: Assisted Coordination
- Phase 4: Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure

**Subject:** Operational depth of OSLO's interaction with execution systems.

**Status:** Doctrine and Constitution are aligned. No reconciliation required.

**Long-Term Direction (separately governed):** Doctrine 10's Long-Term Direction section names Portfolio Cognition, organizational systems cognition, cascading dependency awareness, organizational drift analysis, and cross-initiative consequence modeling as future-direction capabilities. These remain provisional long-term capabilities and are not ratified stages on any axis.

---

## Relationships Between Axes

The four axes are:

- **Distinct.** Each axis has a separately identifiable subject, origin point, and end state. Position on one axis does not mechanically determine position on another.
- **Correlated.** Organizations typically advance along multiple axes together. Trust Gradient often tracks Execution Depth; Cognition Scope often tracks Product Identity.
- **Convergent at terminal.** Three of the four axes name "Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure" as their terminal state. Trust Gradient terminates at "Orchestration Authority," which is the trust-dimensional articulation of the same endpoint.
- **Independently measurable.** An organization may be at Stage 3 of Trust (operational influence granted) while at Phase 1 of Execution Depth (observational only); these states are not redundant.

The framework neither asserts nor denies a mechanical mapping between stage numbers across axes. Phase 0 (Execution Depth) and Stage 1 (Cognition Scope, Product Identity) are at structurally parallel starting positions but are not declared equivalent.
