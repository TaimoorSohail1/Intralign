# Constitutional Drift Warnings

## Warning 1 — Chat-Centric Drift

If conversation becomes the primary experience,
OSLO risks collapsing into generic AI assistant tooling.

---

## Warning 2 — Workflow Drift

If workflow/task management becomes primary,
OSLO risks collapsing into traditional PM software.

---

## Warning 3 — Automation Drift

If automation supersedes understanding quality,
OSLO risks sacrificing epistemic integrity for speed.

---

## Warning 4 — Simplicity Drift

If simplification hides meaningful ambiguity,
OSLO risks false certainty.

---

## Warning 5 — Governance Bureaucracy Drift

If governance becomes procedural rather than epistemic,
OSLO risks feeling like compliance software.

---

## Warning 6 — Hyperactive Intelligence Drift

If OSLO becomes overly interruptive or noisy,
users will experience cognitive fatigue and trust degradation.

---

## Warning 7 — Confidence Absolutism Drift

If confidence appears magical or unexplained,
trustworthiness collapses.

---

## Warning 8 — Artifact Dominance Drift

If artifacts become the conceptual center,
OSLO loses its understanding-centered philosophy.

---

## Warning 9 — Organizational Consensus Drift

If organizational belief alone becomes truth,
OSLO loses independent interpretive integrity.

---

## Warning 10 — Invisible Intelligence Drift

If OSLO recedes too completely,
users lose understanding of intelligence provenance and governance.
