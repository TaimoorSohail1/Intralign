# Strategic Initiative (OKR) — EU Market Expansion (Sample)

> _Sample project (project_type: `strategic_initiative`). A **fictitious worked example** so you can see OSLO analyze a realistic strategic plan immediately. Edit it to fit your initiative, or start fresh._

## Objective
Establish **Acme SaaS** as a credible player in the EU market by end of the fiscal year — a compliant, localized offering with a repeatable EU go-to-market motion.

## Key Results
- Grow EU ARR from **€0 → €1.2M**.
- Sign **40 EU customers**, with ≥10 in the DACH region.
- Achieve **GDPR + EU data-residency** compliance (audited).
- Localize product + billing for **3 languages** (DE, FR, ES) and EUR pricing.

## Context & Rationale
Inbound interest from EU prospects has tripled year over year, but deals stall on data-residency and invoicing-in-EUR requirements. Two competitors already have EU data centers. Expanding now protects against losing the segment and diversifies revenue beyond the home market. This ladders up to the company goal of 40% international revenue within two years.

## Scope
**In scope**
- EU data hosting + GDPR program, EUR billing + VAT handling, localization (3 languages), an EU-focused sales play, and a regional partner or two.

**Out of scope**
- Opening a physical EU office, non-EU international markets, and any M&A.

## Workstreams & Milestones
- **Compliance (Owner: Legal/Sec):** DPA templates Q1; EU data region live Q2; audit Q3.
- **Product (Owner: Eng):** data-residency routing Q2; localization Q2–Q3.
- **GTM (Owner: Sales):** EU pricing + EUR billing Q2; first 10 logos Q3; 40 logos Q4.

## Dependencies & Risks
- *Dependency:* EU data region depends on the cloud-infra roadmap — confirm capacity in Q1.
- *Risk:* compliance audit slips and blocks enterprise deals — start the audit track early, in parallel.
- *Risk:* localization quality undermines credibility — use native reviewers, not machine translation alone.

## Stakeholders
- **Sponsor:** CEO
- **Initiative owner:** VP International
- **Workstream leads:** General Counsel, VP Engineering, VP Sales (EU)
- **Informed:** Finance (FX/VAT), Board (quarterly)
