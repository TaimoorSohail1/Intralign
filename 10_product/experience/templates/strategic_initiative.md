# Strategic Initiative Plan (OKR)

> _Starter template (project_type: `strategic_initiative`). Replace each italic prompt with your specifics, then start analysis. Delete any section that doesn't apply._

## Objective
_State the single qualitative objective this initiative pursues — ambitious, clear, and time-bound (e.g., "Become the default planning tool for mid-market teams this year")._

## Key Results
_List 2–5 measurable key results that prove the objective is met. Each should be a number with a target and a baseline (e.g., "Grow activated teams from 50 → 300")._

## Context & Rationale
_Why this, why now? The strategic reasoning, the problem or opportunity, and how this connects to higher-level goals._

## Scope
**In scope**
_The workstreams and outcomes this initiative owns._

**Out of scope**
_Adjacent work this initiative will not own or deliver._

## Workstreams & Milestones
_Break the initiative into workstreams; for each, note the owner and key milestones/dates._

## Dependencies & Risks
_What this initiative depends on (other teams, funding, decisions) and the top risks to achieving the key results, with mitigations._

## Stakeholders
_Sponsor/owner, workstream leads, contributors, and who must stay informed or aligned._
