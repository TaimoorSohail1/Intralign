# Marketing Campaign — "Brew & Co" Spring Cold Brew Launch (Sample)

> _Sample project (project_type: `marketing_campaign`). A **fictitious worked example** so you can see OSLO analyze a realistic campaign plan immediately. Edit it to fit your campaign, or start fresh._

## Campaign Goal
Drive awareness and trial for Brew & Co's new **Spring Cold Brew** line, hitting **8,000 first-time online orders** in an 8-week window (**Apr 1 – May 26**) and growing the email list by **15,000** subscribers.

## Audience
Existing DTC coffee drinkers aged 25–40 who buy premium beans online, plus a lookalike acquisition segment. They care about taste, sourcing/sustainability, and convenient subscriptions; they spend most attention on Instagram, TikTok, and email.

## Channels & Messaging
- **Paid social (IG/TikTok):** "Cold brew, warm conscience" — taste + sustainability; CTA *Try the Spring trio*.
- **Email:** launch announcement → trial offer (20% first order) → replenishment nudge.
- **Influencer:** 6 mid-tier coffee creators with discount codes.
- **Organic/content:** brew-guide reel series.

## Scope
**In scope**
- Creative production (video + static), 3 email flows, paid social setup, influencer coordination, landing page.

**Out of scope**
- Retail/in-store, out-of-home, and any rebrand of the core line.

## Timeline & Milestones
- **Mar 18** — Creative locked
- **Apr 1** — Launch (paid + email #1 + influencers live)
- **Apr 22** — Mid-campaign optimization checkpoint
- **May 26** — Campaign ends; **May 31** — wrap report

## Budget Assumptions
Total **$120,000**: paid social **$70k**, influencers **$25k**, creative production **$15k**, tools/landing **$10k**. Assumes a blended **CAC ≤ $12** and a 20% trial-offer redemption.

## Success Metrics
- 8,000 first-time orders (primary)
- +15,000 email subscribers
- Blended CAC ≤ $12; paid social CTR ≥ 1.2%; trial→repeat rate ≥ 25%
