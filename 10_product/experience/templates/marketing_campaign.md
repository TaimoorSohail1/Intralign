# Marketing Campaign Plan

> _Starter template (project_type: `marketing_campaign`). Replace each italic prompt with your specifics, then start analysis. Delete any section that doesn't apply._

## Campaign Goal
_What is this campaign trying to achieve? State the primary goal in one or two sentences (e.g., awareness, leads, signups, launch support) and the timeframe._

## Audience
_Who are you trying to reach? Describe the target audience/segment, what they care about, and where they pay attention._

## Channels & Messaging
_Which channels will you use (email, social, paid, content, events, PR)? What is the core message and call to action per channel?_

## Scope
**In scope**
_Deliverables and activities this campaign includes._

**Out of scope**
_What this campaign won't cover._

## Timeline & Milestones
_Key dates: build, launch, key sends/drops, mid-campaign check, wrap. Rough dates are fine._

## Budget Assumptions
_What budget is assumed, and how is it allocated across channels? Note any assumptions or constraints._

## Success Metrics
_How will you measure success? List the target metrics (e.g., reach, CTR, CPL, conversions, ROI) and their targets._
