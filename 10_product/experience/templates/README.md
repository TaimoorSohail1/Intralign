# Release 1 Template Catalog (owner-curated source)

Owner-curated template bodies for **Start From Template** (DL-056). Each file is the **pre-authored planning document** copied into a new project as one editable Artifact (`source="template"`) when its template is chosen. Engineering renders the picker and copies the chosen body in — it authors no content.

**Status:** 🟡 Proposed starter copy — pending owner ratification (DL-056). Body copy may be refined by the owner before GA without a new decision (DL-056 conditions).
