## 1. Trust / honesty as the GTM wedge

**Thesis.** "Trust is the scarcest resource in the AI era." OSLO's **epistemic honesty** — never overstates, shows what it doesn't know, **advise proposes, never disposes** — is a **counter-position** against confident-but-wrong AI. Critically, it's **doctrinally enforced**, so it's a brand promise *backed by the architecture*, not a marketing claim you can be caught faking. In high-stakes planning, that's the word-of-mouth driver (best-in-class SaaS leans **2–3× harder on advocacy** than peers).

- **Candidate positioning line:** *"The planning intelligence that tells you what it doesn't know."*
- **Why durable (the moat under the message):** the honesty is enforced by the epistemic invariants — Derived-never-Attested, confidence/limit disclosure, no fabricated assessment, the DL-048/UP-4 honest-limit disclosure. A competitor running LLM-wrapper confidence can't credibly copy it.
- **Grounding:** Doctrine 04 (confidence = trustworthiness of understanding, not prediction certainty); the Wave E Disclose epistemic-safety contract; DL-055 (advise candidate-only).
- **Expand at:** pre-Beta / Beta positioning + messaging; tie to the CSAT/advocacy loops (feedback spec) and the share-out artifact (G4) so the honesty shows in every shared output.

## 2. Sequence GTM to the Trust-Evolution ladder (land-and-expand)

**Thesis.** Doctrine 09's **Trust Evolution** — observations → recommendations → operational influence → orchestration authority — *is* a land-and-expand ladder, and it maps cleanly onto the DL-075 lifecycle and the adoption loops. Each rung raises switching cost; GTM motion should follow the rungs rather than sell the top of the ladder first.

| Trust rung (Doctrine 09) | Adoption motion | Loop / canon |
|---|---|---|
| Trust **observations** | individual aha — the 60-second orientation | DL-073 onboarding; Planning stage (DL-075) |
| Trust **recommendations** | team alignment — bring in the stakeholders | G3 (CRR invites); Advise (DL-047/055) |
| **Operational influence → orchestration** | org / portfolio embeddedness | G1 (plan→outcome loop), execution monitoring (Pro+); Validation stage (DL-075); Doctrine 10 |

- **Motion:** bottoms-up individual wedge → team collaboration → org/portfolio expansion, priced along the tier ladder (DL-074) and maturing along the Execution-&-Orchestration doctrine (Doctrine 10).
- **Expand at:** GTM motion design and sales/PLG-handoff sequencing, when GTM is actively built out.
