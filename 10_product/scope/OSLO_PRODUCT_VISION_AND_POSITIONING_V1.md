## 1. Mission — Intralign (the company)
Build **AI-native systems that help organizations achieve better outcomes** through **governed planning, execution, and continuous outcome orchestration.**

## 2. Product Vision — OSLO (3–5 year end state)
OSLO becomes the **system of record for outcome integrity** — the **governance and orchestration layer above human and AI execution systems.**
*(Grounded: outcome integrity = coherence between Intended and Current Reality, DL-002; OSLO advises/governs, never acts autonomously, DL-043/047.)*

## 3. The Problem / Why Now
Organizations increasingly **delegate planning and execution to AI**, but **lack mechanisms to ensure understanding, alignment, feasibility, confidence, and outcome integrity.** AI **accelerates execution while amplifying interpretation risk** — making **governed cognition** necessary.

## 4. Category & Positioning
**Strategy: create a new category.**
- **Primary category:** **Outcome Orchestration.**
- **Secondary description:** AI-Native Planning Intelligence and Outcome Governance.
- **Competitive set:** Jira · Asana · Monday · Smartsheet · Microsoft Project · Planview · Primavera · AI planning copilots.
- **Wedge:** **60-second project intelligence** and **governed planning quality assurance.**
- **Entry → expansion:** enter through **planning quality / planning intelligence**, then expand into **execution intelligence** and **outcome orchestration.**

*(Grounded differentiator: epistemic honesty — never fakes certainty/health, never acts; confidence = maturity not probability. Brand posture: strategic · executive · AI-native · trustworthy · premium · outcome-focused, CHG-068.)*

## 5. Target Customer / ICP
**ICP:** professionals responsible for **planning, coordinating, governing, or delivering project-based work.**
- **Initial beachhead:** Project Managers · Program Managers · PMO Leaders · Project Coordinators · Delivery Managers · Transformation Leads.
- **Org size:** mid-market and enterprise.
- **Initial buyer:** individual project professionals seeking better planning quality and project confidence.
- **Future economic buyer:** individuals, teams, and departments responsible for project-based work, regardless of title — PMOs · Transformation Offices · Operations · IT · Product · Business Transformation · Enterprise Change · Strategy Execution Offices.
- **Buying criterion:** **responsibility for successful project outcomes, not possession of a project-management title.**

## 6. Value Proposition by Persona
- **Project Manager:** exposes hidden assumptions/ambiguities/interpretation gaps before execution; improves planning quality + outcome confidence; governed recommendations + issue detection; raises likelihood of successful delivery.
- **Program Manager:** consistent understanding framework across projects; surfaces alignment/feasibility risk early; portfolio-level outcome-confidence visibility; governance without admin burden.
- **PMO Leader:** scales planning governance org-wide; measurable planning-quality standards; improves portfolio confidence + decisions; outcome-oriented governance capability.
- **Business Leader / Executive Sponsor:** visibility into confidence/alignment/feasibility; identifies hidden risks pre-execution; better investment/prioritization; confidence that initiatives support intended outcomes.
- **Transformation / Change Leader:** shared understanding across stakeholders; detects interpretation drift as initiatives evolve; improves alignment; strengthens execution vs strategy.
- **Product Leader (future):** clarity before execution; aligns strategy↔planning↔delivery; reduces rework from misunderstanding.

## 7. Strategic Objective Ladder
1. **Improve individual project outcomes** →
2. **Improve planning intelligence** (expose assumptions/ambiguities/risks/interpretation gaps before execution) →
3. **Establish governed planning** (systematic governance over understanding quality, confidence, alignment, feasibility) →
4. **Establish outcome intelligence** (org visibility into outcome confidence + execution readiness) →
5. **Enable outcome orchestration** (continuously govern outcome integrity as work/context/understanding evolve) →
6. **Become the outcome system of record** (authoritative governance layer above human + AI execution) →
7. **Establish Outcome Orchestration as a discipline** (create and lead the category).

*(R1 sits at Levels 1–2: prove planning intelligence + first value; R1 = owner validation vehicle, CHG-064.)*

## 8. Differentiation / Moat
Governance model · Outcome Intelligence corpus · Trust telemetry · Outcome Confidence framework · behavioral-data network effects · proprietary interpretation-risk datasets · cross-project outcome reasoning. *(Anchored by the governed cognitive architecture + epistemic invariants — structurally hard to copy.)*

## 9. Non-Goals (what OSLO is NOT) — high-confidence (canon)
OSLO is **not**: an autonomous agent that acts on artifacts (DL-047 — advises only); a project "health/readiness score" tool (epistemic fail-conditions); a probability/prediction engine (confidence ≠ probability); a governance/Authority engine in R1 (deferred); **and not a work-coordination tool** — it **governs the quality of understanding outcomes depend on**, it does not "manage"/coordinate the work itself (this is why "Outcome Management" is retired — DL-050).

## 10. Positioning Statement
> **For** business leaders and project professionals responsible for delivering outcomes in increasingly AI-driven environments, **OSLO is** an **outcome orchestration platform** that **validates understanding, alignment, feasibility, and confidence before work begins and continuously governs outcome integrity as work evolves.** **Unlike** project management, planning, and execution tools that primarily coordinate work, **OSLO governs the quality of understanding that successful outcomes depend upon.**

## 11. Strategic chain & onboarding positioning (DL-087, 2026-07-02)

Ratified user-facing positioning frame (DL-087; **presentation/positioning only** — no structural, doctrinal, or object change):

- **The strategic chain — Understanding → Judgement → Decision → Oversight.** **OSLO supplies the Understanding** (Perceive→Infer→Evaluate → CAF · Reliability · Confidence · MRI · Findings); the human brings **Judgement, Decision, and Oversight**. OSLO *supports / records / surfaces* these — it never performs them (reinforces advisory-only, DL-043/DL-047).
- **Spine:** *OSLO makes you a more strategic PM* — framed as enabling an **AI-first PM** (augmented, never on autopilot; an augmentation claim, distinct from internal "AI-First delivery").
- **Product descriptor (user-facing):** **"Strategic project leadership"** (the internal descriptor *Planning Intelligence* / Outcome Orchestration category is unchanged).
- **Outcomes language (bounded):** copy may say the user *steers toward the outcomes they want* — outcomes are **human-attributed**; OSLO never predicts, delivers, or guarantees outcomes (Confidence ≠ success / health / probability).
- **Control-anchored copy (future-safe):** promises anchor on human control ("you decide; you stay in control; nothing changes without you"), **not** capability negations ("never runs your project") — true across the execution roadmap.
- **Plain-language labels** (Plan sections, Work breakdown, Clarity · Alignment · Feasibility, Initial / Extended Analysis) and the **"Judgement" (human act) vs the banned "Judgment Layer"** disambiguation are registered in `00_owner/CANONICAL_GLOSSARY.md` Disambiguation Register (DL-053).
