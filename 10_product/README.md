# Product

This domain holds product-shaped content: user experience, PLG flows, workflows, collaboration model, and tier behavior. Content here is derived from Doctrine and Constitution and is operationalized for product application.

## Content

- `user_experience/` — Workspace layout, wireframes, navigation, interaction rules.
- `plg/` — Product-led growth flows including the 60-second activation flow.
- `workflows/` — User-facing workflow specifications.
- `collaboration/` — Collaboration and sharing logic.
- `tiering/` — Freemium and tier behavior.

## Precedence Posture

Product content is **derived** from Governance content. It is subordinate to Doctrine and Constitution. Each product spec should cite the doctrinal or constitutional source it implements. Concepts introduced here without doctrinal anchor are provisional pending governance promotion.

## Citation Rules

Refer to `01_governance/doctrine/` for foundational claims and `01_governance/constitution/` for operational principles. Product specs operationalize doctrine; they do not assert it.
