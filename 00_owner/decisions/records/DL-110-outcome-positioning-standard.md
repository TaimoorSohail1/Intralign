# DL-110 — Outcome-positioning standard — understand & steer toward outcomes, never forecast

- **Date:** 2026-07-13 · **Status:** Ratified · **Decided by:** Idris (Founder Console)
- **Class:** A

This decision **consolidates and standardizes** OSLO's outcome-positioning language into one canonical statement plus a banned register. It introduces **no** new capability, object, or invariant; it registers, as a single reference, an outcome-language standard that is currently correct but **scattered** across DL-043/047 (advisory-only), DL-087 (strategic chain; bounded, human-attributed outcomes), DL-107 + `RELEASE_1_REPORTING_SPECIFICATION_V1` ("frame by outcome, never forecast"), the Outcome Confidence Interpretation Doctrine (confidence = understanding maturity, not probability), DL-104 (P1 health-framing defect), and the Product Vision non-goals ("not a predictor, not a health score"). Positioning / doctrine-orientation only.

## Decision

Adopt the **Outcome-Positioning Standard** as the single canonical articulation of what OSLO offers *toward outcomes*:

> **OSLO lets you understand and steer toward outcomes with honest, revisable confidence — it does not forecast them.**

Owner-preferred marketing form (preserve verbatim): *"…let you understand and steer toward outcomes with honest, revisable confidence, not forecast them."*

Reading of the terms (all grounded in ratified canon):
- **understand** — OSLO supplies the *Understanding* leg of the strategic chain (DL-087); measured as understanding maturity, reliability-qualified.
- **steer toward outcomes** — the human steers; outcomes are **human-attributed** (DL-087 bounded-outcomes; advisory-only DL-043/047). OSLO instruments the steering; it never performs it.
- **honest, revisable confidence** — Confidence = understanding maturity, read with its basis; it **evolves and may fall** as understanding matures (Outcome Confidence Interpretation Doctrine). Never success probability / health / readiness.
- **not forecast them** — OSLO **frames by outcome, never forecasts the outcome** (DL-107; Reporting spec §91). "Not a predictor" (Vision non-goal).

**Banned register (mechanically discouraged in user-facing copy, reporting, onboarding, and sales):** "predict / forecast your outcomes," "probability / likelihood / chance of success," "will your project succeed," "outcome guarantee," "on track to succeed," project **health / readiness / RAG** scores. Any surface a reader could mistake for probability-of-success / health / readiness remains a **P1 defect** (DL-104).

This standard is **binding on positioning and messaging** across surfaces (product copy, onboarding, reporting, marketing, sales) as the reconciled single source; the distributed statements above remain valid and are now indexed to it.

## Conditions

1. **Consolidation, not new claim.** Reaffirms DL-043/047, DL-087, DL-107, DL-104, the Interpretation Doctrine, and the Vision non-goals; supersedes none. No structural, capability, or invariant change.
2. **Human-attributed outcomes.** Copy never presents OSLO as predicting, delivering, or guaranteeing outcomes; the human steers and decides (advisory-only).
3. **Confidence stays maturity.** "Confidence" in any outcome-positioning copy is understanding maturity, read with its basis; never success probability / health / readiness.
4. **Banned register enforced.** The banned terms above are disallowed in user-facing surfaces; the P1 health-framing/overclaim guards (DL-104) apply to any outcome surface.
5. **Verbatim marketing line preserved.** The owner-preferred phrasing is retained exactly as the approved marketing form.
6. **Positioning-only.** Lands as an addition to `10_product/scope/OSLO_PRODUCT_VISION_AND_POSITIONING_V1.md` (positioning) + a `CANONICAL_GLOSSARY` standard/disambiguation entry ("Outcome-Positioning Standard"). Internal descriptors (Planning Intelligence / Outcome Orchestration) unchanged.

## Supersedes / Amends

Additive. **Amends** the Product Vision positioning (adds the consolidated standard) and **adds** a `CANONICAL_GLOSSARY` entry indexing DL-043/047/087/107/104 + the Interpretation Doctrine + Vision non-goals to one line. **Supersedes no prior decision.**
