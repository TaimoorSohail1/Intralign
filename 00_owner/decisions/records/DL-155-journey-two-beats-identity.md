## Decision

The Overview journey hero gains its **two real beats on the main axis**:

> **Understand → ⟮ Optimize: Validate · Improve ⟯ → Execute** — *on the way to the outcome.*

- **Understand** — a **first-time progression milestone**, not a dwelling stage. OSLO's read lands; the user's **first optimization action** crosses them onward, and it shows **done** thereafter (a "you've begun" marker, never re-earned).
- **Optimize** — a **bracket** spanning the two working beats, keeping the north-star word on the axis:
  - **Validate** — confirm OSLO's load-bearing inferences so the read becomes **trustworthy**. Its metric is **execution-readiness coverage** (e.g. *7 of 23*).
  - **Improve** — act on the now-trustworthy read to lift what it flags weakest. Its metric is the **maturity read** (Outcome Confidence — band · CAF · limiter).
- **Execute** — the **destination**: hand the plan to the execution tool. **Always reachable** (DL-145 §4), never active, never a "ready" verdict.

**Each node owns exactly one metric**, so the coverage count and the maturity band — which read as redundant when crowded onto a single node — become **sequential stops with a real threshold between them**.

## Why two beats — and why they are never merged

The owner asked whether coverage could simply *replace* the read ("progress through the 23 items"). It cannot, and this DL fixes the line in canon: **coverage and maturity can move in opposite directions.** Confirm that the venue Wi-Fi supports 150 (not the inferred 500) and **coverage rises** while **Feasibility falls or holds** — you validated the plan and found a wall. So **you can reach 23 of 23 and still be Moderate**: full coverage means the read is fully *grounded* (reliable), not that the plan is *good*. Merging them into one "% done" number would re-assert the false-confidence forecast the product exists not to be (**D183b / D180**) and undo the level-vs-trust split (**Option C**). The two beats give the user the sequence — *make the read true, then make the true plan good* — **without** collapsing the two axes.

## The trigger — behavioural, computed, and a fix

The old rule flipped Understand→Optimize when the **band reached High** — backwards, since optimizing is what *raises* the band. Replaced with **computed, behavioural** transitions (D173): **Understand → Validate** on the user's **first optimization action** (the first-time reward — immediate, earned); **Validate → Improve** when the **load-bearing inferences are grounded** (the read is reliable), *not* when the band is High. **Honest reward (D088):** crossing a node and ticking coverage happen immediately; the maturity band still moves only at an analysis update — the reward is the journey advancing, never a fake read bump.

## Two structural calls this identity DL ratifies

- **The read is a PERSISTENT hero panel (§6A).** Outcome Confidence stays the always-visible first panel (**D179a**) below the arc — the beats change *what you act on*, never whether the read is shown. Rejected: surfacing the read only on the Improve beat (it would hide the hero). This supersedes DL-153's "the read is the Understand node's detail": the read is the through-line the whole journey raises, not one node's property.
- **Start here follows the beat (§6F).** The issue list re-ranks by what the current beat needs — **Validate** leads with what **grounds the read**, **Improve** leads with the **limiter dimension** — so the beats are *functional*, not cosmetic labels. Computed (D173), **advisory and non-blocking** (a focus sequence, never a gate on Execute), no tally (D179e).

## What stays intact · what changes

**Stays:** Outcome Confidence unchanged as the maturity read (D199, D174); **Confirm** as the per-item verb (D196a) — *Validate* is the stage; non-blocking Execute; no health / no forecast / no composite (D003 / D183b); one home per count (D179e); the read is always the first panel (D179a).
**Changes:** *Optimize* becomes a bracket over **Validate · Improve** (two axis nodes, one metric each); **Understand** becomes a first-time milestone; the transition trigger becomes **behavioural**; the read is affirmed **persistent** (not pinned under Understand); **Start here becomes beat-aware**.

## Why this is a Class-A call

The arc's stages are what the hero says the product *does*, step by step — identity, the same reason DL-152/154 were Class A. Splitting the middle into Validate → Improve, and making the read the through-line rather than one node's detail, changes the shape of that self-description. It lands as a ratified decision; the build (four-node render, the threshold, beat prioritisation, the guard changes) follows as **Class B**.

## Forward note — extends into execution monitoring

The four-stop axis is built to **extend past Execute** into the execution-monitoring direction (*Execute → In execution → Outcome*), each stage opening into its own detail exactly as the beats do here. The anti-health spine (D003 / D183b) carries across the boundary. The per-beat-detail pattern this DL establishes is what makes that extension cheap rather than a rebuild.
