# DL-079 — Ratify the third-party evaluation-lens cognition boundary (E4): governed input, never silent disposition

- **Layer:** Governed epistemic core (Evaluate · Advise) — **doctrine-level boundary**, drawn **within** the existing epistemic model. **Affirms (does not alter)** the epistemic invariants; introduces no new invariant.

## Decision — the boundary
1. **A third-party evaluation lens is *governed input data to first-party cognition* — never cognition a third party performs.** OSLO controls all application. A lens may cause OSLO to surface an **attributed, confidence-qualified consideration / flag / candidate Issue**, but it **never alters CAF / confidence / scores** and **never disposes** (*advise proposes, never disposes*; **OB-5** — only reanalysis changes assessment).
2. **Phasing: reference-only first, governed overlay as north-star.** **Option A (attributed advisory reference via Disclose) is the first step**; **Option B (governed Derived overlay OSLO applies) is the north-star target.** **Option C (certified third-party contribution to Evaluate *scoring*) is NOT adopted** and is **fenced as a separate, deliberate future doctrine decision** — it is the only path that would put third-party content inside the scores, and it is not opened by this decision.
3. **Epistemic status of a lens.** A lens is a **governed Derived overlay**, not Attested truth and not third-party cognition. **Never silent:** every lens effect is **attributed** (named) and **confidence-qualified**.
4. **CAF stays first-party.** CAF (Clarity / Alignment / Feasibility — DL-062), confidence, and all scoring are **not extensible by third-party lenses** under this decision (changing that would require the fenced Option C doctrine decision). Reliability drivers stay decomposable.
5. **Governance regime (realization).** Any lens must be **calibrated / validated and auditable before it may flag.** Lens authors are external `Principal`s (DL-049); the **certification bar, attribution, and liability bounds** are set in the E4 realization (engineering/governance **proposes**; owner **ratifies** — ratify ≠ author). The **artifact-profile registry (DL-078)** may host lenses under the **same L2-structure-not-L3-cognition** governance.
6. **E4 stays design-only.** This ratifies the **boundary**, not an E4 build. No E4 capability is built until its realization is proposed under this boundary.
7. **Invariants binding (affirmed).** Attested / Derived; **OB-5**; *advise proposes, never disposes*; **first-party CAF**; no fabricated assessment; honest-limit disclosure.

## Opportunity (why this direction)
Keeps the **governed-cognition trust differentiator** — "the marketplace that *can't* manipulate your judgment" — fully intact while still admitting **governed domain expertise**. It is the safe path toward the deepest moat (the E4/E6 domain-expertise ecosystem): the moat is pursued **without** ever letting a third party silently move an OSLO assessment.

## Realization (follow-on)
Engineering/governance **proposes** the lens mechanism under this boundary — application as a governed Derived overlay, attribution + confidence-qualification, the calibrate-before-flag regime, certification, and attribution/liability bounds — for owner ratification. **E4 remains design-only until then.**
