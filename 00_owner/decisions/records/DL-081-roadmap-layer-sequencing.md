## Decision
Ratify the **Layer-Before-Depth** roadmap sequencing principle: OSLO establishes cross-platform planning-governance **breadth** at the understanding/governance altitude **before** descending into execution-phase governance **depth**. Altitude (being *above* multiple systems) and neutrality — not phase coverage — constitute the "layer." Realization order:

1. **Prove planning-governance value** — §7 levels 1–2 (R1). Unchanged; R1 stays narrow (CHG-064, DL-076); may be single-context.
2. **Establish cross-platform neutrality** — planning-intelligence governance supports **≥2 meaningful execution/planning platforms** (e.g., Jira · Asana · MS Project) as governed sources, with real per-platform depth. This constitutes the layer (altitude + neutrality) and is the anti-collapse moat incumbents are structurally disqualified from holding.
3. **Then descend into execution intelligence / execution governance** — §7 levels 4–5 — governing execution occurring *in those external platforms*, from the established altitude, carrying the accumulated interpretation-risk corpus (§8).
4. **Execution-phase read-only visibility** (outcome observation) is permitted earlier as a feedback input, **distinct from** execution governance.
5. **Advisory-only invariant (DL-047) preserved throughout** — "execution governance" governs/observes execution in external systems; OSLO never executes or coordinates work itself.

## Conditions
1. **Sequencing/orientation only.** Sequences existing §7 ladder levels and the DL-034 Execution Depth axis; introduces no new doctrine, structure, capability, or responsibility.
2. **Advisory-only preserved.** "Execution governance" never means OSLO acting; the DL-047 invariant survives the entire descent (Positioning §9).
3. **Neutrality threshold, not an integration commitment.** "≥2 platforms" is the bar for claiming layer/altitude; specific platform targets and timing remain product-scope authorship (RELEASE_MODEL_AND_ALPHA_LADDER, OSLO_LINEAR_INITIATIVES).
4. **R1-safe.** Does not alter R1 scope (CHG-064, DL-076); applies to R2+.
5. **Doctrine boundary (Anti-Assumption).** Dispositioned as product-roadmap canon per owner selection. If the sequencing is later to carry doctrinal force (Execution Depth, Doctrine 10), it must be re-routed as a doctrine change — not assumed under this record.
6. **Monitored assumption.** Durability assumes the governance layer yields outcomes the bare model can't and that buyers pay for an external auditable layer; recorded as a watch-item, not a guarantee.
