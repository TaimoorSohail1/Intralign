## Rationale

The governance stabilization effort initiated with the Context Plane reconciliation work successfully resolved the iteration overhead problem that motivated the introduction of OGAP v1.0 and DECISION-READY. The three integration items identified in the OSLO Repository Stabilization and Governance Consolidation Audit (Phase 5 readiness assessment) were each ratified in the Audit's recommended Phase 6 sequence: DL-039 (GOV-CP-001B at 19/20), DL-040 (OGAP at 18/20), DL-041 (DECISION-READY at 18/20).

The Integration Moratorium served its purpose by preventing new mechanism proposals during the integration sequence. With integration complete, the Moratorium's continuation would be a permanent block on future governance machinery without serving its original stabilization purpose. DL-042 closes the Moratorium explicitly and confirms that future acceleration mechanisms require ratification through the standard governance process (Framework 001, Framework 001A, OGAP v1.0, DECISION-READY).

The transition from stabilization phase to operational governance state shifts focus from governance-on-governance work to substantive governance work in the architecture, doctrine, ontology, and contract domains. The recent governance sequence has been notably meta-governance-focused (Context Plane classification, authority attribution, protocol adoption, state amendment); DL-042 marks the formal close of this period.
