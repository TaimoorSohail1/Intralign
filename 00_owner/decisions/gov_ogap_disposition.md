## Decision

The repository owner ratifies the adoption of the OSLO Governance Acceleration Protocol (OGAP) v1.0 as a Protocol (not a Framework) operative under DL-040. The full OGAP v1.0 text is recorded at `01_governance/protocols/ogap_v1.md`.

## Selected Adoption Vehicle

**Protocol Category** — OGAP v1.0 is ratified as a Protocol, distinct from Frameworks (which are governed by the CLAUDE.md operative Governance Discipline directive "Do not create new Governance Frameworks"). The Protocol category is introduced by this Decision as a parallel governance instrument that operates over Framework 001 and Framework 001A without modifying either.

---

## Clarifications (As Ratified)

DL-040 carries five clarifications, all attached to the ratification:

**Clarification 1 — OGAP is a Protocol category, not a new Framework.**
The Protocol category is introduced as a distinct governance instrument class. OGAP v1.0 is the first member of this category. Future Protocols may exist alongside Frameworks 001 and 001A and any subsequent Frameworks, subject to owner ratification on a per-Protocol basis. The Protocol category does not displace the Framework category.

**Clarification 2 — OGAP does not modify Framework 001 or Framework 001A.**
Framework 001 (governance lifecycle: Backlog → Proposal → Review → Decision → Repository Change → Changelog) and Framework 001A (Review output schema and Review states) remain in force unchanged. OGAP does not alter their text, their authority, or their operational scope.

**Clarification 3 — OGAP operates as a routing and acceleration protocol layered over Framework 001 and Framework 001A.**
OGAP Stages 1–5 (Triage, Discovery, Sufficiency Gate, Readiness Score, Stop Rule) insert before Framework 001's Proposal stage as discovery and gating discipline. OGAP Stage 6 standardizes Proposal structure via Type A–E templates. OGAP Stages 7–9 narrow Review focus and confirm owner-only ratification authority, consistent with Framework 001A and CLAUDE.md. The Stop Rule prevents redundant reconciliation cycles. OGAP's role is to route governance items through the existing Frameworks more efficiently.

**Clarification 4 — The CLAUDE.md prohibition on creating new Frameworks remains unchanged.**
The operative Governance Discipline directive ("Do not create new Governance Frameworks") continues to apply to Frameworks. OGAP is not a Framework; the directive does not apply to OGAP. The directive is not modified, lifted, or extended by DL-040.

**Clarification 5 — OGAP is authorized because it is owner-ratified as a Protocol, not because AI created a new governance Framework.**
The adoption authority is owner ratification. AI contribution to OGAP's creation was limited to drafting per owner direction and articulating the discipline during prior governance items. The Protocol's authority derives from owner ratification (DL-040), not from AI initiation.

## Adoption Scope

**What DL-040 establishes:**

- A new governance instrument category: Protocol.
- OGAP v1.0 as the first ratified Protocol.
- OGAP's nine stages (Governance Triage → Discovery Governance → Evidence Sufficiency Gate → Decision Readiness Assessment → Decision Ready Rule → Proposal Generation → Review → Owner Decision → Disposition Processing) as operative governance discipline.
- Decision Type Templates A–E as standardized Proposal structures.
- Governance Stop Rule preventing redundant reconciliation cycles.

**What DL-040 does not do:**

- Does not modify Framework 001 or Framework 001A.
- Does not modify the CLAUDE.md Governance Discipline directive on new Frameworks.
- Does not modify any existing canonical content beyond appending to the Decision Log, the Changelog, and adding the new protocol artifact + disposition document.
- Does not adopt GOV-FWK-001A-DECISION-READY (which depends on OGAP being canonical and remains a separate pending decision).
- Does not change the canonical layer stack, doctrine, constitution, or any contract.
- Does not lift the Integration Moratorium directive (the Moratorium permits processing of pending items; OGAP is one of those pending items being processed).

## Procedural Effects

**Status of Framework 001.** Unchanged. Lifecycle stages remain as defined. OGAP Stages 1–5 operate before Framework 001's Proposal stage; OGAP Stages 6–9 align with Framework 001's Proposal → Review → Decision → Repository Change → Changelog Entry sequence.

**Status of Framework 001A.** Unchanged. Review output schema (Findings / Concerns / Dependencies / Recommendation / Status) and Review states (Accepted / Accepted with Conditions / Rejected / Deferred / Returned for Revision) remain operative. OGAP Stage 7 constrains Review focus to five areas (evidence quality, scope correctness, contradictions, dependencies, risks) but does not modify the schema or states.

**Status of CLAUDE.md.** Unchanged. The Governance Discipline directive continues to operate. CLAUDE.md may benefit from a future minor housekeeping update to acknowledge the Protocol category and OGAP v1.0; that update is not bundled into DL-040 and would require separate owner direction.

**Status of GOV-FWK-001A-DECISION-READY (DECISION-READY state amendment).** Unchanged by DL-040. With OGAP now canonical, DECISION-READY's dependency on OGAP mechanics (Decision Readiness Score, Evidence Sufficiency Gate, Stop Rule) is satisfied. The amendment becomes processable as a separate decision per the Phase 6 sequencing recommendation from the OSLO Repository Stabilization Audit.

**Status of Integration Moratorium.** Partially satisfied. Two of three Decision Ready items (GOV-CP-001B as DL-039; OGAP v1.0 as DL-040) are now integrated. One Decision Ready item remains (GOV-FWK-001A-DECISION-READY). The Moratorium continues to apply to new mechanism proposals beyond those already pending.

**Status of GOV-CP-001 (paused).** Unchanged by DL-040. Remains paused per DL-038 Condition 4.

## Rationale

The OSLO Repository Stabilization Audit (Phase 5 Candidate 1) scored OGAP at 18/20 (Decision Ready). The two objective blockers identified were:

- B1.1 — Adoption vehicle selection.
- B1.2 — Governance Discipline directive interaction.

DL-040 resolves both through Clarifications 1–5. The adoption vehicle is the newly-introduced Protocol category (Clarification 1). The Governance Discipline directive interaction is resolved by classifying OGAP as Protocol rather than Framework (Clarifications 4 and 5), leaving the directive unchanged.

The Phase 6 sequencing recommendation placed OGAP at Step 2 (after GOV-CP-001B and before DECISION-READY). DL-040 honors this sequencing.

The empirical case for OGAP is the iteration overhead documented across the GOV-CP-000 → GOV-CP-001A → GOV-CP-001B sequence. OGAP's stages address each contributing cause: Triage prevents proposal-type drift; Discovery/Decision separation prevents premature drafting; Sufficiency Gate and Readiness Score prevent weak proposals; Stop Rule prevents re-analysis of decidable issues; Templates standardize structure.
