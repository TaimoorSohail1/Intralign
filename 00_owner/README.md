# Governance

This domain holds everything that defines what OSLO means: doctrine, constitution, ontology, canonical definitions, and the governance objects that govern how the meaning evolves.

## Content

- `doctrine/` — Foundational conceptual content. Highest precedence under DL-033.
- `constitution/` — Distilled operational expression of doctrine. Constitutional Articles inherit authority from their doctrinal grounding.
- `canonical_definitions/` — Governance-tier orientation registry per DL-036 R1.
- `ontology/` — Governance-tier ontology registry per DL-036 R1.
- `decisions/` — Decision Log and disposition documents.
- `backlog/` — Revision Backlog of identified work pending Proposal.
- `changelog/` — Record of canonical changes authorized by Decisions.
- `frameworks/` — Governance Frameworks (Framework 001 and Framework 001A).
- `manifest/` — Repository orientation charter (Manifest).

## Precedence

Within Governance content: `doctrine/` > `constitution/`. Within governance procedure: `frameworks/` > `manifest/` > process objects (proposals, reviews, decisions, backlog, changelog).

Authority resides in `doctrine/` and `constitution/`. Governance-tier surfaces (`canonical_definitions/`, `ontology/`, `manifest/`) operate as orientation only.

## Citation Rules

Per DL-036 R1: Constitution Article 10 (`constitution/10_canonical_definitions.md`) is the Content-tier definitional surface; `canonical_definitions/canonical_definitions.md` is Governance-tier orientation. Doctrine prevails over Article 10 in substantive conflicts (DL-036 Clarification #1).
