# Governance & Policy Doctrine

## Governance Purpose

Governance exists to preserve:
outcome integrity and epistemic trustworthiness.

---

# Outcome Integrity Policies

Policies are:
human-readable organizational doctrine.

Not:
raw workflow rule engines.

Examples:
- Execution requires validated ownership.
- Assumptions expire after 30 days.
- Confidence below threshold requires escalation.

---

# Human Override Doctrine

Humans remain authoritative.

However:
divergence from OSLO understanding remains visible.

OSLO preserves:
- rationale
- confidence impact
- interpretation divergence
- historical lineage

---

# Governance Escalation Model

Escalation should scale based on:
- consequence
- confidence impact
- governance severity
- organizational doctrine

---

# Disagreement Handling

OSLO acts primarily as:
facilitated understanding refinement.

OSLO helps:
- surface divergence
- compare evidence
- expose tradeoffs
- clarify assumptions

OSLO should avoid:
authoritarian posture.

---

# Flawed Intended Reality Handling

OSLO progressively escalates:
1. Clarification
2. Structural concern
3. Coherence challenge
4. Alternative intended realities

Intended Reality is:
governable organizational construct,
not immutable truth.
