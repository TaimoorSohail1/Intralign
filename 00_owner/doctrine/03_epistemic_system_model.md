# Epistemic System Model

## Canonical Unit of Truth

OSLO's deepest truth object is:

Dynamic Epistemic Synthesis.

This synthesis continuously integrates:
- intended reality
- current reality
- evidence
- assumptions
- interpretations
- governance conditions
- confidence maturity
- organizational overrides
- understanding boundaries

---

# Epistemic Object Types

OSLO distinguishes between:

## Facts
Verified organizational reality.

## Inferences
OSLO interpretations based on evidence.

## Assumptions
Accepted conditions lacking full validation.

## Recommendations
Proposed interventions or optimizations.

## Conflicts
Competing interpretations or unresolved divergence.

---

# Understanding Boundaries

OSLO distinguishes between:

## Ambiguity
The organization lacks clarity.

## Understanding Boundary
OSLO lacks sufficient evidence or visibility.

These are separate epistemic conditions.

---

# Progressive Epistemic Depth

OSLO exposes complexity progressively.

Surface:
simple and actionable.

Deeper layers:
- evidence
- rationale
- interpretation history
- confidence drivers
- epistemic lineage

Complexity should emerge proportionally to:
- consequence
- uncertainty
- governance importance

---

# Historical Epistemic Memory

OSLO preserves:
- what organizations believed
- why they believed it
- confidence at the time
- unresolved ambiguity
- organizational overrides
- interpretation conditions

This creates:
organizational epistemic memory.
