# Outcome Integrity Framework

## Intended Reality

Represents:
what the organization believes should exist.

Examples:
- success criteria
- strategic intent
- governance expectations
- target outcomes
- operational assumptions

---

## Current Reality

Represents:
what OSLO currently believes actually exists based on evidence.

Examples:
- feasibility conditions
- ambiguity
- stakeholder divergence
- execution telemetry
- assumption instability

---

# Integrity Gap

Outcome Integrity measures the degree of coherence between:
- Intended Reality
and
- Current Reality

Drift emerges when:
Current Reality diverges from Intended Reality.

---

# Confidence Model

Confidence is not:
prediction certainty.

Confidence represents:
trustworthiness of current organizational understanding.

Confidence dimensions include:
- clarity
- alignment
- feasibility
- evidence strength
- interpretation stability
- governance integrity

---

# Confidence Maturity

Possible maturity progression:
- Initial
- Expanded
- Validated
- Continuous

Confidence maturity reflects:
depth and quality of understanding.

---

# Outcome Integrity States

Examples:
- Initial
- Clarified
- Aligned
- Feasible
- Governed
- Execution Ready
- Fragile
- Drift Emerging

These are:
epistemic and governance states,
not workflow phases.
