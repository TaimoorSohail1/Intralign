# Organizational Cognition Model

## Strategic Arc

OSLO evolves through four major cognition layers:

1. Individual Cognition
2. Shared Understanding
3. Governed Organizational Cognition
4. Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure

---

# Individual Cognition

Initial user experience focuses on:
- orientation
- confidence
- ambiguity reduction
- feasibility understanding
- understanding refinement

Primary emotional experience:
"I understand my initiative more clearly."

---

# Shared Understanding

Teams progressively collaborate around:
- interpretations
- assumptions
- ambiguity
- governance
- intended outcomes

OSLO becomes:
shared cognition infrastructure.

---

# Governed Organizational Cognition

Organizations begin governing:
- understanding quality
- confidence conditions
- assumptions
- drift
- escalation
- outcome integrity

Governance becomes:
epistemic governance.

---

# Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure

Long-term evolution:
OSLO becomes:
- orchestration substrate
- governance environment
- organizational cognition layer
- agent governance infrastructure

This includes:
- cross-system orchestration
- portfolio cognition
- policy systems
- outcome governance
- dynamic coordination

---

# Shared Understanding Model

OSLO preserves:
- evolving understanding
- disagreement
- rationale
- assumptions
- confidence evolution
- historical epistemic state

The goal is not merely:
alignment.

The goal is:
governable shared understanding.
