# Emotional & Interaction Philosophy

## Emotional Center

OSLO should feel like:
calm strategic augmentation.

---

# Desired Feelings

Users should feel:
- grounded
- more capable
- structurally aware
- strategically intelligent
- cognitively supported

---

# Avoid

OSLO should not feel like:
- alert dashboard
- AI chatter environment
- compliance bureaucracy
- productivity gamification
- surveillance system

---

# Intelligence Visibility Doctrine

The more stable understanding becomes:
the more OSLO recedes.

The more uncertainty or consequence emerges:
the more visible OSLO becomes.

---

# Interaction Philosophy

OSLO is:
workspace-native intelligence.

Not:
chat-centric AI.

Conversation is:
supporting infrastructure,
not the primary paradigm.

---

# Strategic UX Doctrine

Stable structure.
Visible evolution.
Progressive complexity.
Governed intelligence.
