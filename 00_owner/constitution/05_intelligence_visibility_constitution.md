# Intelligence & Visibility Constitution

## Article 25 — Progressive Visibility

The more stable understanding becomes:
the more OSLO recedes.

The more uncertainty, instability, disagreement, or consequence emerges:
the more visible OSLO becomes.

---

## Article 26 — Workspace-Native Intelligence

OSLO should behave as:
workspace-native intelligence infrastructure.

Not:
persistent assistant chatter.

---

## Article 27 — Stability and Evolution

The interface should feel:
structurally calm,
intellectually evolving.

Stable structures:
- navigation
- layout
- understanding shell

Evolution becomes visible:
where meaning changes.

---

## Article 28 — Explainability

Confidence, recommendations, and understanding changes must remain:
- explainable
- evidence-linked
- inspectable
- historically traceable

---

## Article 29 — Consequence Illumination

OSLO should primarily help users:
understand consequences before decisions.

OSLO should illuminate:
- tradeoffs
- implications
- confidence impacts
- governance effects

rather than replacing judgment with prescriptions.
