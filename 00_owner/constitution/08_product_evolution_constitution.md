# Product Evolution Constitution

## Article 40 — Progressive Identity Evolution

Organizations should progressively experience OSLO as:

1. Planning and understanding intelligence
2. Strategic cognition environment
3. Governance infrastructure
4. Outcome orchestration infrastructure

> **Governance Note (per DL-034 / RB-003):** Under the ratified OSLO Evolution Framework, this Article articulates the **Product Identity** axis. The canonical doctrinal source for this axis is Doctrine 09. At Stage 3, Doctrine 09's label "**Governed Organizational Cognition**" prevails over the label "Governance infrastructure" used above. The Article's Stage 3 label is **provisional** and superseded by the doctrinal label per DL-033 doctrinal precedence. The Article body is not amended; the doctrinal label governs operational use.

## Article 41 — Trust Evolution

Organizations first trust:
- OSLO observations

Then:
- OSLO recommendations

Then:
- OSLO operational influence

Then:
- OSLO orchestration authority

## Article 42 — PLG Orientation

The onboarding experience exists to:
- orient users
- generate insight
- establish trust
- create understanding
- expose integrity conditions

The onboarding experience is not:
the full workspace model.

Execution interaction evolves progressively:

Outcome orchestration infrastructure

## Article 44 — Organizational Cognition Expansion

OSLO progressively evolves:
- individual cognition
- shared understanding
- organizational cognition
- portfolio cognition
- orchestration infrastructure

> **Governance Note (per DL-034 / RB-003):** Under the ratified OSLO Evolution Framework, the canonical **Cognition Scope** axis is the four-stage arc in Doctrine 02 (Individual Cognition → Shared Understanding → Governed Organizational Cognition → Outcome Orchestration Infrastructure). Doctrine 02's four-stage arc prevails over the five-stage arc enumerated above per DL-033 doctrinal precedence. **Portfolio Cognition** is dispositioned as a **provisional long-term capability**, not a ratified stage on the Cognition Scope axis, pending separate governance. It is anticipated by Doctrine 10's Long-Term Direction section but is not doctrinally promoted to a stage. The Article body is not amended; the doctrinal arc governs operational use.
