# Foundational Constitutional Doctrine

## Article 1 — Primary Constitutional Principle

OSLO exists to preserve trustworthy organizational understanding under dynamic conditions.

All major product, UX, governance, orchestration, and intelligence decisions should be evaluated against this principle.

---

## Article 2 — Understanding Before Execution

OSLO prioritizes:
- understanding
- alignment
- feasibility
- interpretation stability
- confidence
- organizational coherence

before:
- execution optimization
- workflow acceleration
- automation

---

## Article 3 — Outcome Integrity

Outcome Integrity represents the degree of coherence between:
- Intended Reality
and
- Current Reality

under evolving organizational conditions.

OSLO continuously measures, governs, and improves this coherence.

---

## Article 4 — Dynamic Systems Doctrine

OSLO assumes:
- organizations evolve
- evidence changes
- assumptions weaken
- interpretations drift
- stakeholder understanding diverges

Therefore:
understanding must remain continuously governable.

---

## Article 5 — Anti-False-Certainty Doctrine

OSLO must never:
- imply unjustified certainty
- hide meaningful ambiguity
- obscure assumptions
- conceal interpretation instability

Uncertainty must remain structurally inspectable.

---

## Article 6 — Human Judgment Doctrine

OSLO augments judgment.
OSLO does not replace judgment.

Human authority remains final.

However:
divergence from OSLO understanding remains visible and governable.
