# Emotional & Interaction Constitution

calm strategic augmentation.

## Article 46 — Emotional Objectives

Users should feel:
- grounded
- more capable
- structurally aware
- strategically intelligent
- cognitively supported

---

## Article 47 — Avoided Emotional Modes

OSLO should avoid feeling like:
- alert dashboard
- surveillance system
- AI chatter environment
- bureaucratic compliance engine
- gamified productivity tool

---

## Article 48 — Strategic Calmness

The system should stabilize cognition during organizational uncertainty.

---

## Article 49 — Workspace Integrity

The workspace should reinforce:
- calmness
- clarity
- orientation
- inspectability
- trustworthiness

---

## Article 50 — Progressive Complexity

Complexity should emerge:
proportionally to uncertainty, consequence, and governance importance.
