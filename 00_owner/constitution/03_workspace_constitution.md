# Workspace Constitution

## Article 12 — Understanding-Centered Workspace

Understanding is the center of the system.

Artifacts support understanding.
Understanding does not support artifacts.

---

## Article 13 — Outcome Space

The primary operating object is:
Outcome Space.

An Outcome Space represents:
the evolving synthesis of organizational understanding surrounding intended outcomes.

---

## Article 14 — What OSLO Understands

The primary workspace should center on:
What OSLO Understands.

This includes:
- confidence
- integrity state
- intended vs current reality
- understanding evolution
- key concerns
- recommendations
- governance conditions

---

## Article 15 — Artifact Layer

Artifacts remain:
important but subordinate.

Primary artifact domains:
- Intent
- Context
- Execution
- Schedule
- Resources

---

## Article 16 — Adaptive Workspace

OSLO should remain:
conceptually stable,
operationally adaptive.

The workspace adapts based on:
- integrity condition
- unresolved concerns
- governance state
- user role
- organizational context

---

## Article 17 — Persistent Confidence Shell

Confidence is:
persistent epistemic infrastructure,
not a report.

Confidence and integrity state should remain globally visible.

---

## Article 18 — Companion Intelligence

Conversation exists:
contextually.

Conversation supports understanding.
Understanding should not support conversation.
