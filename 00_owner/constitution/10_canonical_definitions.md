> **🔎 Two canonical-definitions surfaces exist (KIA2-3 / DL-036).** This is the **Content-tier — authoritative operational expression** (within the Constitution). The companion is the **Governance-tier orientation registry** at `01_governance/canonical_definitions/canonical_definitions.md`. **Where they conflict substantively, Doctrine prevails (DL-036 Clarification #1).** For an operational definition, use this file; for the orientation registry / cross-index, use the governance-tier file.

## Outcome Space

A governed workspace representing the evolving synthesis of organizational understanding surrounding intended outcomes.

---

## Outcome Integrity

The degree of coherence between Intended Reality and Current Reality under evolving organizational conditions.

---

## Intended Reality

What the organization believes should exist.

---

## Current Reality

What OSLO currently believes actually exists based on evidence and interpretation.

---

## Integrity Gap

The degree of divergence between Intended Reality and Current Reality.

---

## Dynamic Epistemic Synthesis

The continuously evolving synthesis of:
- evidence
- assumptions
- interpretations
- governance conditions
- intended reality
- current reality
- confidence
- organizational overrides
- understanding boundaries

---

## Confidence

The trustworthiness of organizational understanding.

---

## Confidence Maturity

The maturity and quality of the system's understanding and evidence foundation.

---

## Understanding Evolution

The longitudinal evolution of organizational understanding over time.

---

## Organizational Epistemic Memory

The preserved historical record of:
- what the organization believed
- why it believed it
- confidence conditions
- unresolved ambiguity
- governance conditions
- rationale
