# Intralign System Overview — One-Page Summary

## 

## **Outcome-Driven Strategic Lifecycle Orchestration (OSLO)**

**Intralign** is an AI-native planning and outcome governance platform that enables organizations to design, align, and coordinate delivery across human and machine actors while remaining execution-tool neutral.

Intralign governs **outcome integrity**, ensuring execution continues to serve intended business outcomes as conditions evolve.

---

## **Core System Architecture (High Level)**

```
Planning Systems
        ↓
   Outcome Graph (Knowledge Layer)
        ↓
      OSLO Layers
        ↓
Execution Platforms & Actors
```

Intralign sits **above execution systems**, providing planning intelligence, governance, and coordination rather than replacing delivery tools.

---

## **Key Intralign System Components**

## **1) Planning & Workflow Systems**

User-facing systems where delivery plans are created and structured.

Planning functions include:

- Outcome and project definition
- Scope and requirements structuring
- Workflow & methodology selection
- Resource & schedule planning
- Governance setup
- AI-assisted artifact creation

Planning establishes outcome-aligned delivery structure.

---

## **2) Outcome Graph (Knowledge Layer)**

The canonical structure representing relationships between:

- Outcomes
- Initiatives & workstreams
- Execution work
- Human & AI actors
- Decisions
- Constraints & assumptions
- Execution & validation signals

The Outcome Graph serves as:

- shared coordination memory
- reasoning substrate
- execution alignment structure

All OSLO reasoning operates over this structure.

---

## **3) OSLO Layer Functions**

Intralign intelligence and governance capabilities emerge from the interaction of OSLO layers.

### **Knowledge Layer Functions**

Maintain authoritative system state:

- Outcome Graph storage
- Relationship persistence
- Evidence linkage
- Temporal history & snapshots
- Canonical state resolution

This layer represents enterprise delivery reality.

---

### **Reasoning Layer Functions**

Analyze state to detect issues and opportunities:

- Planning quality analysis
- Gap detection
- Alignment reasoning
- Risk & drift detection
- Outcome viability assessment

Produces analytical outputs.

---

### **Judgment Layer Functions**

Convert reasoning outputs into actionable guidance:

- Recommendation generation
- Tradeoff analysis
- Option evaluation
- Decision proposals

Outputs candidate actions.

---

### **Governance Layer Functions**

Authorize and constrain delivery actions:

- Policy enforcement
- Outcome protection rules
- Decision authorization
- Execution constraint enforcement

Ensures outcome integrity is preserved.

---

### **Communication Layer Functions**

Coordinate humans and systems:

- Evidence-carrying outputs
- Human decision support
- Notifications and explanations
- Alignment communications

Ensures decisions are explainable and actionable.

---

### **Execution Layer Functions (Future Expansion)**

Coordinate execution actions while remaining execution-platform neutral:

- Routing approved changes to tools
- Coordinating work across actors
- Supporting human & agent execution

Execution tools continue to manage tasks.

Intralign governs outcomes.

---

## **4) Execution Integration Infrastructure**

Connects Intralign to systems where work is executed.

Initial integrations include:

- Jira
- Asana
- Microsoft Planner / Project

Capabilities:

- Plan export
- Bi-directional synchronization
- Execution status ingestion
- Change tracking

Execution tools remain unchanged.

Intralign adds intelligence and governance.

---

## **5) Ecosystem & Marketplace Systems**

Enable experts and partners to monetize delivery expertise.

Includes:

- Workflow marketplace
- Solution pack ecosystem
- Delivery partner programs
- Vendor routing partnerships

Partners expand platform adoption.

---

Intralign is not another project management tool.

Intralign becomes:

**The governance and coordination system between planning, execution, and outcomes across humans and machines.**
